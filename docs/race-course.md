# Race course and Racers (0.8.0)

A **race course** is a property of a run (`docs/runs.md`): the player marks a red or black run, puts a **Start Hut** beside its top and a **Timing Hut** beside its foot, and **Racers** come to train on it in winter. Ships ON with no flag; the plain `Bot` builds no hut, so no racer ever spawns in a balance run. Owner decisions (2026-11-16): the huts unlock at **level 3** (the World Cup stays at level 4), gates are placed **automatically only** (no drag tool), a course run **stays open to other skiers** on training days.

Source: `src/config/race.ts` (every number), `src/sim/race/RaceCourse.ts` (system), `src/render/race/RaceCourseView.ts`, `src/render/meshes/buildings/raceHuts.ts`, the Racer row in `src/config/guestTypes.ts`.

## Buildings
| Building | Size | Cost / upkeep | Unlock | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Start Hut (`startHut`) | 1 x 1 | $6,000 / $12 a day | level 3 | beside a piste, footpath or trail (`access: pisteEdge`, never ON a piste tile); no smoke, no power |
| Timing Hut (`timingHut`) | 2 x 1 | $14,000 / $30 a day | level 3 | same rules; carries the live board (two posts above the roof) |

Upkeep is lower than the plan's first guess ($40 / $90): the first year of `npm run analysis:race` put the course $20k a year in the red; see "Economy" below.

## Course validity (`RaceCourse.reasonFor`)
A run can be a course when it is a single clear run (not a loop, not a blob), **red or black** (`difficulty >= 3`), has a drop of at least **60 m**, has a Start Hut within **2 tiles** (footprint gap) of one of the first 3 centre-line tiles and a Timing Hut within 2 tiles of one of the last 3. At most **3** courses. The reasons are plain text (`RACE.reasons`): *Needs a red or black run*, *Needs a drop of at least 60 m*, *Needs a single clear run, not a loop*, *Needs a Start Hut at the top*, *Needs a Timing Hut at the foot*, *Three courses is the limit*.

A marked course whose hut is bulldozed (or whose run is edited below the rules) goes **inactive** and says why; it is never dropped silently. Run ids persist (`Names.pisteId`), so the flag survives growing / splitting / merging a run (settings of vanished runs are pruned).

## Gates (auto)
`courseGates(run, size)` is a pure function of the run: every N centre-line tiles (**red 2, black 1**: the plan said 3 / 2, but a run on a 64-tile map is only ~10-20 tiles long), alternating sides (+-0.6 tile from the line) and alternating red / blue flags. Not saved, not obstacles for skiers. Rendered by `RaceCourseView`: ONE `InstancedMesh` of poles and ONE of flags for all courses (14 triangles a gate), plus ONE board mesh (an atlas of 3 slots, two back-to-back quads each): **3 draw calls** in total, about 4,000 triangles with three black courses. The board ("BLACK RUN 2 / BEST 1:12.4 / RACERS 7") redraws at most once per sim hour or when a lap lands; the best time is cosmetic (`RACE.boardBaseS` + length / `boardMps` with a seeded jitter), the sim never reads it.

## Racers (guest type index 14)
- Winter only (`winter: 0.06`), level 3, `requires ['@raceCourse']` (pseudo id from `Spawner.flagsFn`: at least one open, valid course), segment `adventure`, fallback `adventure`, group 1-3, budget $180-360, expert skills, yellow bibs and helmets.
- **Demand** = the arrival weight times `RaceCourse.demand(racersAlive)`: the summed **quality** of up to 3 open courses (groomed within a day 1.0, two days 0.7, otherwise 0.4; times 0.8 for red, 1.0 for black); **0** without an open course or once `maxRacersPerCourse` (12) racers per course are alive. A closed course (by you, the weather or no snow) gives quality 0: racers stop coming and the ones already there ski normally.
- **Behaviour**: at a lift top `Exploration.pickVia` is restricted to the tiles of the open courses (`racerTiles`), the descent is ~1.4 x faster (`NavGraph.speed` `skiMul`), reaching the via tile is a **lap** (`GuestSim.raceLap` -> `RaceCourse.onLap`); the **first lap of a day pays the course fee** ($20, income category `recreation`); after 3-6 laps (stable per guest id: `lapQuota`) the racer leaves when the run ends (`raceDone`, stateless: read from the saved lap record). Racers never stop for a coffee (`coffeeBreak`) or a Schirmbar drink (`slopeStop`), but eat at lodges as every guest does.
- Course on a **black** run is worth more: quality 1.0 against 0.8, so more racers, laps and fees (and, from 0.8.1, a bigger World Cup, see below).

## Saves
The flag lives in the `runs` module (**v2**, optional `course` array of run ids; absent while there is none, so hashes of resorts without a course are unchanged; v1 saves load with no courses). Lap records, the day's racer count and the board times are the `raceCourse` module (v1, `omitWhenNull`, part of `stateHash` because the laps decide when a racer leaves). `tests/persist/raceSave.test.ts` saves in the middle of the training, reloads and runs on for two days identically to the control. `SAVE_FORMAT_VERSION` is unchanged.

## UI
Run inspector: a **Make a race course** / **Not a race course** button (44 px, `data-role="run-course"`) with the one-line status under it (the reason when it cannot be marked, *Race course · 4 racers today · best 1:02.3* when it is). **Operations -> Runs** has a *Course* column (a flag toggle per run, the reason as tooltip). Placing the huts uses the normal building drawer (Recreation). Phones: the toggle lives in the run inspector sheet; no new floating card.

## Advisor, achievement
Rudi: `race.course.available` (Start Hut unlocked, a red / black run with 60 m of drop exists, no course yet, a settled resort with the money for both huts) and `race.course.ungroomed` (an open course with a quality below 0.5). No goal or chapter depends on a course. Achievement **Through the gates** (`through-the-gates`, fact `racerLaps`: 100 laps in one game).

## Economy
`npm run analysis:race` (`scripts/analysis/raceRoi.ts`) runs the starter resort for a 180-day year with and without a course on the main run (same seed). Result of the shipped numbers (64 maps, seeds where the starter run can be a course: 61, 5): a **black** course nets about **+$1,000 a year** (income +$8,200 from fees and the racers' meals, upkeep +$7,300), a **red** one about **-$6,800 a year**: the huts are a cost centre that pays back (slowly) only for a groomed black run. Against the 59-row generic table in `docs/economy-audit.md` that is mid-to-low (ranks 26-35 of 59), well below lifts, lodgings and hotels, never in the top third. The first draft (upkeep $40 + $90, share 0.03, fee $14) was -$19k to -$23k a year.

## Race days (0.8.1)
Source: `RACE_DAY` in `src/config/race.ts` (every number), `src/sim/race/RaceDay.ts` (system), `src/config/raceSponsors.ts` (the sponsor pool), `GuestSim.sendSpectators`, `RaceCourseView` (the board), the *Race day* card in `StEvents`.

**Club Race.** In **Marketing -> Events -> Race day** the player books a Club Race on one of the marked courses: **$3,000**, **2 days of notice**, **Saturday or Sunday only**, **winter only**, **one per calendar week** (Monday to Sunday), level 3 (the huts' level), refused while a World Cup covers the day. Cancelling before the day refunds 80 %. The booking pays into the `marketing` ledger like a festival (`RaceDay` is not a festival row: it needs a course and its own date rules, so `FESTIVAL_IDS` and the four event tables did not change; news goes through the plain `toast`).
- **10:00** (`time:hour`): if the course is valid and open the race starts: the run is **closed to the public with the reason "Race day"** (`OpenStatus.closeTiles`, 40 ticks, so it opens again at 14:00 by itself and survives a save), the Racer demand drops to 0, a field of **20-30** (seeded by (seed, day, course)) is at the gate. A course that is closed, inactive or unmarked at 10:00 **cancels the race and refunds the whole fee** with a news item. Guests skiing on the run when it closes walk off like in any closure.
- **Spectators** are **existing guests**, no new type: at 10:00, 11:00 and 12:00 up to 16 more guests (nearest to the Timing Hut first, ties by id, no RNG) who are idle or walking about (not riding, queueing, skiing, on an excursion, leaving, sleeping or injured; no racers, no children of a family) walk to the Timing Hut with a normal `Intent` and stand around its door (a stable hash of the guest id spreads them over ~1.6 tiles, never on a building, lift or water tile) until 14:00 (`GuestSim.spectateUntil`). The cap is **18 (red) / 28 (black)** for a club race, **32 / 40** for the World Cup, never above 40 (`RACE_DAY.maxSpectators`). At 14:00 each spectator is a bit hungry (`hungerAfter` 0.35), so the lodges, cafes and kiosks around the finish sell the extra food and drink through the normal food decision; no new income path.
- **14:00**: results. The finishing times are a **pure function of (seed, day, course, racer)** times the course quality (`raceTimes`; no shared RNG is drawn, so a race day never moves another system's stream). The winner (a fictional name, `RACE_DAY.winners`) goes to the **timing board** ("RACE DAY - LIVE" during the race, "WIN 1:12.4" for three days after) and into the **news**. Income: **$10 entry fee a racer** (`recreation`) plus the **sponsor's flat sum** (`other`).
- A club race day draws **x1.10 arrivals** that day only (`RaceDay.arrivalMult`, in the spawner's attractiveness product).

**Sponsors.** A **pool of ten made-up, harmless Alpine names** (owner decision 2026-11-16; `SPONSORS`: a cheese dairy, a ski-wax maker, a mountain bakery, honey, pine tea, cowbells, fondue, goggles, sausages, socks). A course gets **one sponsor**, picked by a seeded hash the first time a race day is booked on it (never one another course already has while the pool lasts) and kept. It pays **$250-340** a race and adds **0.7-1.0 rating points (at most +0.05 stars)** through a new optional `eventBonus` rating input that **fades linearly over 14 days**. The board shows its name; the news names it, and three of the ten have one dry line (humour sparing). `tests/repo/raceSponsors.test.ts` checks every name against a denylist of real brands (and a human reviews the list when it changes; no name may be a real company, a brand or resemble one).

**World Cup on the course.** The festival now needs `['lodge', 'raceCourse']` (a valid course on a red **or black** run; the old "black piste" rule remains only for fixtures without the race wiring). On each of its three days the same 10:00-14:00 race runs on the best course (black first): field 36-48, $20 entry fee, spectators up to 32 (red) / 40 (black), results, sponsor. A **black course** also lifts the festival's arrival multiplier by x1.08 (`worldCupBlackMult`). The festival dressing uses the **course line** for the racers and puts the grandstand + finish banner at the foot of the course, beside its Timing Hut. A save with a World Cup already booked and no course keeps the old behaviour (the abstract black-piste dressing, no race session).

**Economy guard.** `npm run analysis:race -- --raceday 1` runs two identical resorts per seed to a winter Saturday, one with a booking, and compares that day's income: **x0.99-1.34 a normal Saturday** on the 64 maps (guard: 1.5). The $3,000 fee is a marketing expense: a race day does not pay for itself, like a festival; it is for the atmosphere, the rating nudge and the extra meals.

**Saves.** The `raceCourse` module is **v2**: next to the laps it holds `bookings`, the running `session` (course, field, spectator ids, sponsor), `results` (the last six), `sponsors` and the rating `bonus`; it is still `null` (absent from the save and `stateHash`) in a resort without a booking, a World Cup race or a sponsor, and a v1 save loads with no race day (`tests/persist/raceDaySave.test.ts`: a save in the middle of a race reloads identically, runs on for 120 ticks identically and finishes with the same result).

**Tests.** `tests/sim/race/raceDay.test.ts` (booking rules, closure 10:00-14:00, cancel + refund, pure times, one sponsor per course), `spectators.test.ts` (walk to the hut, cap, hunger, rating bonus bounded and fading), `worldCup.test.ts`, `tests/persist/raceDaySave.test.ts`, `tests/ui-dom/raceDay.test.ts`, `tests/render/raceCourseView.test.ts` (board lines, still 3 draw calls), `tests/repo/raceSponsors.test.ts`. `npm run race:sanity` now also books a race day, looks at the closure, the crowd, the results, saves in the middle of the race and checks a World Cup day on the course.

## Downhill course (0.8.2)
The summer twin: a **mountain-bike trail** (Layer.Mtb) can be a **downhill course** with the same Start Hut and Timing Hut. Source: `RACE.mtb` in `src/config/race.ts`, `RaceCourse` (`mtbRuns`, `idOf`, `activeMtb`, `demandMtb`, `mtbGoalTiles`), a second `RunIndex` over the MTB layer (`sim.mtbRunIndex`), the Downhiller row in `src/config/guestTypes.ts`.
- **Validity** is the ski rule: single clear trail (not a loop), red or black by steepness, drop >= 60 m, Start Hut within 2 tiles of the first 3 centre-line tiles, Timing Hut near the last 3 (huts accept `trail` access). Reasons read "trail" (`RACE.mtb.reasons`). Up to three per kind (ski and downhill count separately). The trail is **open** while every tile is bikeable (no snow, not closed).
- **Ids and names**: a trail has no Names id, so a course id is `RACE.mtb.idBase + a tile of the trail` stored in `RunSettings.course`; it follows the trail while that tile stays on it. **Names** are "Downhill N" with a number kept per course (`raceCourse` module, optional `mtbNo`, assigned when the course is marked, the lowest free number): unmarking another course renumbers nothing, and a save without numbers is numbered by id order.
- **A removed trail** (no trail holds the anchor tile any more) leaves the course **inactive with the reason** "The trail is gone: rebuild it or unmark the course" (a placeholder entry in `RaceCourse.courses()`, shown in the Runs tab, the Events picker and bookable never); it comes back by itself when the trail is rebuilt over that tile, and can be unmarked at any time.
- **Marking**: Operations -> Runs lists red / black trails as rows ("(trail)"), the Course column reads "Downhill". No grooming, no manual closing.
- **Reachability**: a downhill course must be **reachable from a bike lift**: `CourseInfo.blocked` ("Riders cannot reach the top: connect it to a bike lift") when no bike-carrying lift gets a rider to one of the first 3 centre-line tiles. Two checks: a snow-independent flood from the lift tops over trails (ridden downhill or level: bikes cannot climb) and footpaths / roads, and, while the course is open, the real bike path search. The Runs row reads "Downhill course, not usable: ...", Rudi says "Riders can't reach the top of Downhill N" (`race.downhill.unreachable`), the course draws **no demand** and no Downhiller. Build the trail so it starts at or below the top station of a bike lift and is connected to it.
- **Downhillers** (type 15, summer 0.05, level 3, `@mtbCourse` = a usable downhill course AND an open bike lift): always on the bike, orange jerseys. A rider takes a bike lift up, rides the bike path to the first reachable of the first 3 course tiles (`Decider.mtbCourseStarts`, a stateless pick) and then **rides exactly the course tiles to the foot**: the excursion route is the course centre line from that tile (`RaceCourse.mtbRoute`, `GuestSim` ToTrail). A lap = reaching the foot (`GuestSim.mtbLap`), the first lap of a day pays $14 (`recreation`), 3-5 laps then they leave. Demand = surface 0.9 x difficulty fit (red 0.8, black 1.0), 0 once 10 riders per course are alive. Winter racers and ski courses are untouched (`active()` is ski only).
- **Race day**: Club Race on a downhill course is summer-only, same $3,000 / weekend / one a week, but a smaller race (`RACE_DAY.mtb`: field 8-12, $8 entry, 8 / 10 spectators for red / black, the sponsor pays 60 %), because a summer Saturday earns less: the guard stays <= 1.5x (`analysis:race -- --summer 1`: x1.17-1.38 on seeds 5, 3, 11); the trail is closed to bikers 10:00-14:00 (the bike nav honours the closed mask), the board reads RIDERS, times use `RACE.mtb.boardBaseS / boardMps`, news says "Downhill Race". The World Cup stays on ski courses.
- **Saves**: `raceCourse` v2 adds optional `todayMtb`; a session stores `mtb` only when true; a world without a downhill course serialises exactly as in 0.8.1.
- Tests: `tests/sim/race/mtbCourse.test.ts`, `tests/persist/raceDaySave.test.ts` (downhill), `npm run race:sanity -- --summer 1`.

## Out of scope
A sponsor banner as its own mesh (the board carries the sponsor line: 0 extra draw calls), player-controlled racing, leaderboards (never: no sharing features).

### Downhill economy numbers (0.8.2)
`npm run analysis:race -- --summer 1 [--days 60 --seeds 5,3,11]`: the same resort with and without a downhill course for 60 summer days. **Capex** is the hut pair at list price ($6,000 + $14,000 = $20,000) times the **summer construction discount** (x0.5, `constructionMultiplierAt`), so $10,000 in a summer resort and $20,000 in winter: the script now prints the multiplier. Net over 60 summer days: -$0.7k (black) to -$4.1k (red): a small cost centre, never a money machine. A seed whose lift top is lower than the trail (seed 3 here) shows the "not usable" reason instead of riders.
