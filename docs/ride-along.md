# Ground-level views: ride-along (M5.2)

`src/render/view/`: `ViewMode.ts` (state machine `iso | ride | photo`, `walk` reserved and unreachable), `ViewCameraRig.ts` (PerspectiveCamera rig + pure pose maths), `RideAlong.ts` (target pick, sim -> `RideSample`, despawn detection), `cameraMath.ts` (`lodHeightFor`, `terrainLodHeightFor`, `projectToScreen`, `horizonFraction`). UI: `StViewHud` (HUD + cabin frame), "Ride along" in `StGuestInspector`. Fog: `src/render/Fog.ts`.

## How it works
* **Read-only.** RideAlong only reads the guest store; `stateHash` is identical with ride on/off (tests/render/rideAlong.test.ts runs 3 sim days).
* `Renderer.enterGround('ride')` freezes the iso `CameraController` (`freeze()` returns the exact pose, all iso input is ignored), `exitToIso()` restores it bit-for-bit (`thaw`). `renderer.camera` is the active camera; `renderer.preFrame` positions the rig before every other per-frame consumer.
* **Keys / mouse (ride only):** Esc exit, N next guest, C recentre, drag = look (+-100 deg yaw / +-60 deg pitch, canvas is the drag surface), wheel = FOV 55..90 (default 70). Space + - . , still work. Every other key and every pointer event on the canvas is swallowed in the capture phase (tools / picking / iso camera never see them). Tile picking is therefore off in ride mode.
* **UI:** `body.view-ride` hides every `#ui` child except `st-view-hud` and the (scaled-down) toasts.
* **Pacing:** guests move ~6 tiles per sim tick while skiing (a run is ~0.5 s at 1x). While riding, `loop.timeScale` is multiplied by `RIDE_TIME_SCALE` (0.15): real-time pacing only, the sim ticks identically. The HUD says "slow-mo". Restored on exit.
* **Eye heights** are in the DRAWN figure scale: an adult figure is `FIGURE_HEIGHT` = 0.52 units tall (feet at the origin, 0.55 with a hat; measured from the geometry, tests/render/groundQuality.test.ts keeps the constant honest). The eye is ~0.9 x H above the guest origin so the camera sits at head height of the guests next to you: ski 0.47, board 0.45, foot 0.47, bike 0.50. (Before M5.2 these were 0.98 / 0.95 / 1.02 / 0.92, from an assumed 1.15-unit figure: the camera floated far above every other guest's head.) Cable carriers sit on the cable (`RideDeps.cableY` projects the guest onto the lift path): chair -0.12 (seated riders' heads are at about -0.08 below the grip: the pad is at -0.40, the backrest top at -0.17, footrest -0.58), gondola -0.6 (bench top -0.77, ceiling -0.54), cable car -0.85, funicular +0.8 above the track. Bob (ski 0.008 / bike 0.012), cable sway and carve banking are scaled by `rig.motion` (0 with reduced motion). Critically damped follow (position 0.07 s, heading 0.28 s, pitch 0.35 s), snap when the target jumps > 6 tiles. Near plane 0.05 (the own figure is hidden, the chair is ahead / behind the eye).
* **Fog:** `scene.fog` is ALWAYS a FogExp2 (density 0 in iso, so no recompiles); terrain, water and both tree shaders include the shared `uFogColor/uFogDensity` chunk (`Fog.ts`). The colour is the CSS sky's horizon colour (`Lighting.onHorizon`) and the CSS gradient's horizon stop follows the camera pitch (`Lighting.horizonAt`), so distant terrain melts into the sky. 95 % opacity at `fogRangeFor(size)` (90..260 tiles).
* **LOD in perspective:** `lodHeightFor(camera)` = `top - bottom` for ortho (iso unchanged) and 30 for perspective. Trees use distance bands (full < 40 tiles from small 32-tile cells, low-poly 40..100 from 64-tile cells, billboards beyond; vertex shader cuts outside the band `uLodRange`), rocks < 90. Guests: full figures, no impostor points, culled beyond 120 tiles and behind the camera, the followed guest is hidden (`hideSlot`). Terrain on 256+ maps keeps the far LOD with the chunks within 15 tiles of the camera at full res (`setNearFocus`).
* Hidden in ride mode: smoke, emotes, floating text / particles, overlays, grid, piste highlight, selection ring.

## Seat on the carrier (lift riders)
The sim puts a lift rider on the line axis, but towers stand ON the axis (mast) with sheave trains over the two strands (+-0.3), so an eye on the axis passed through the mast. Cable riders (`EYE[kind].strand`) now sit on the **outer half** of their carrier: eye = axis + right-of-heading x (`STRAND_OFFSET` 0.3 + `seatLateral`). `seatLateral`: chair = the guest's seat among the outer seats (`(j + 0.5) x SEAT_PITCH`, j from a hash of the guest id, at least `MIN_SEAT_OUT` 0.13: 4-seat chair 0.13 or 0.255), gondola 0.15, cable car 0.2. Deterministic per guest (`RideSample.seat` = guest id, `seats` from `liftSeats`), walk-mode rides use a fixed seat. The chair eye is -0.19 below the grip (was -0.12) so the crossarm (-0.13..-0.07) passes overhead. The heading used is the last known one (carriers dwelling at a station keep the offset). Pure tests (tests/render/rideSeat.test.ts) simulate a pass of every tower for chair2 / chair4 / chair6 / gondola / cable car: eye to mast axis >= 0.43, to the sheaves >= 0.14 (chairs), crossarm gap >= 0.09. Before / after sequences: `--view ride --freeze 1 --vjs` with `__slope.view.freeCam` placed from `eyePosition` at distances from a tower (docs/lifts-clearance.md).

## Harness
`npm run shot -- --size 128 --seed 61 --start winter --hour 11 --pre "__slope.populateDemo('medium'); __slope.spawnGuests(300,'skiers')" --ticks 1500 --view ride --guest auto --freeze 1 --out ride-ski-x`
* `--view ride|lift` enters the view after `--ticks`/`--eval` (`lift` waits for a chair rider about half way up). `--guest auto|<id>`, `--vticks N` extra ticks inside the view, `--freeze 1` pauses the sim for a static frame, `--pre` runs JS before `--ticks`. The log shows `[view]` (at entry) and `[view-final]` (after the wait: draw calls, triangles, pose, terrain LOD stats).
* Page API: `__slope.view.{rideAuto(), ride(id), rideLift(family?), exit(), next(), stats(), skiers(), liftRiders(), liftProgress(id), rig, along}`. NB `populateDemo` takes a lower-case level (`'medium'`).

## Close-up LOD (Task 11)
See `docs/models.md` ("Close-up LOD"). While a perspective camera is active: tree / rock bands cross-fade (stipple), trees < 8 tiles are the high-poly variants, the 40 nearest guests use the detailed figure, lifts within 6 tiles use near chairs / cabin interiors / towers and the round cable, station roofs dither away within 3 tiles, terrain gets micro-detail. `__slope.view.freeCam(x, y, z, yaw, pitch)` places the ride camera anywhere (QA); with the harness: `--view ride --freeze 1 --vjs "__slope.view.freeCam(51,5.2,101,-1.4,-0.05)"`.
* Scale note (resolved in Task 13): figures are 0.52 tall and chairs 0.58, so they were always consistent in iso; only the ride rig assumed 1.15. See "Eye heights". Cabins are ~0.4 tall (the gondola interior is shorter than a standing figure; seated riders are inside the solid shell and not drawn, iso unchanged), trees are ~10x a figure: the world is "chibi" scaled, which is deliberate.
* Roofs: ALL building roofs (the `roof` material layer) and lift station roofs dither away within `BUILDING_ROOF_HIDE_RADIUS` = 3 tiles of a ground camera when they are more than 0.15 above the eye (`BuildingMeshes.setNearHide`, uniform `uNearHide`, 0 in iso).
* Pacing setting: Settings > Gameplay > "Ride-along slow motion" (default On, localStorage `slope.ride.slowmo`). Off = the current game speed (a ski run is then ~0.5 s at 1x).
