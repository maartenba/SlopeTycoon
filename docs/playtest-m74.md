# Playtest checklist: M7.4 (village services, sky, Carnival)

Things agents cannot judge. Details: `docs/m74-buildings.md`, `docs/festivals.md`.

- Pizzeria: do families and budget guests stop for pizza, and does the $9 price feel right? Is the oven smoke too much?
- Bakery: is the 06:00 - 12:00 window noticeable, and does the Facilities bonus matter?
- Tourist Office: is +6 % arrivals worth the price at level 2? Only one per resort: is the refusal message clear?
- Grass Strip + Skydive Centre: do the plane and canopies read well from the default camera? Do jumps stop in rain, fog and at night?
- Launch Ramp + Landing Field: are gliders and paragliders visible without being noisy?
- Carnival (6-11 February): does the parade read as a parade? Is the brass band quiet enough? Is the 5 % sales bonus invisible but fair?
- Does the street requirement (6+ road or footpath tiles) explain itself when it blocks the booking?
