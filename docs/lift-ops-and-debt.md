# Lift variants, operations, breakdowns and the debt model (Milestone 3 playtest follow-up)

**Debt** (`sim/Finance.ts`, `config/prices.ts: DEBT`): cash may go negative. Status `ok | debt | bankrupt`. In debt: construction is refused (Economy.charge needs cash), overdraft interest 1.5 %/month is booked
('interest'), rating gets a small multiplier penalty (`Rating` input `debt`), HUD money turns red, the Finance drawer shows a banner. `finance:debtWarning {money,negDays,level}` fires on level changes (1 fresh, 2 >=7 d, 3 >=30 d) and
every 30 days. Only `Finance.declareBankruptcy()` (Finance drawer button + confirm) or the automatic foreclosure ends the game. **Foreclosure is per difficulty** (`DIFFICULTY[..].foreclosureDays / foreclosureFloor`, fix3 "gentler summer"): Relaxed and Normal = never (0; declaring stays the player's call), Challenging = 360 continuous days (two in-game years of 180 days) below -$150k (it was 182 days below -$50k for everybody, which a bad first summer could trigger). `DEBT.autoBankruptcyDays` / `Floor` (360 / $150k) are only the fallback for a bare `Finance`. Overdraft interest and the rating penalty are unchanged:
`finance:declaredBankrupt {reason}` + `finance:bankrupt`, `Economy.locked` (books frozen), loop speed 0 and cannot be restarted. main.ts shows the end screen (stats, Start new game / Keep looking read-only).
A 'Restructure debt' loan offer appears while cash < 0 (ignores the debt cap). Startup grace unchanged.

**Tourism Board off-season support loan** (`SUPPORT_LOAN` in `config/prices.ts`, `Loans.offerSupport/expireSupport`, opened by `Finance.checkDebt`): the FIRST time cash goes negative in summer or autumn the Loans panel gets a one-time offer `support`: interest-free, 12 months (= one 180-day game year), $15-40k (hole + $15k buffer, rounded up to $5k), ignores the debt cap (still counts for the 5-loan limit). It lapses when winter starts, is only offered once per game, taking it does not use up the Starter loan, and it is part of the save (`loans.support`). Ranger Rudi's `cash.debt` advice mentions it while it is open. Rudi also warns in the last 20 days of spring (`cash.summerBuffer`) with the amount to save (30 days of upkeep).

**Off-season relief** (`OFFSEASON_RELIEF` in `config/balance.ts`, applied in `createSim` daily costs; `sim.relief` keeps the dollars saved this session): buildings whose season is `winter` (cannons, ski school, snowcat garage/outpost, après bar, ice rink...) are mothballed outside winter and pay 25 % of their upkeep (closed-for-conditions buildings still pay 50 %); a venue that was closed or served nobody for 3 days in a row puts its staff on furlough at 50 % wage; lifts that carry nobody (no riders on the line, empty queue) draw only 35 % of their running energy (`LIFT_OPS.idleEnergyShare`).

**Lift variants** (`config/lifts.ts`): tbar, chair2, chair4, chair4d (detachable), chair6, chair6b (bubble), chair8b, gondola6, gondola10, cablecar, funicular. Legacy ids `chairlift`->chair4, `gondola`->gondola6 (`normalizeLiftKind`,
applied in LiftStore.add/validateLift/placeLift). Throughput = carriers/hour x capacity (`LiftStats.carriersPerHour/capacity`). Stations cost `stationCost` each (x2). Extra defs: comfort (ride happiness), windTolerance, energyPerHour,
upkeepFactor, wearRate, loadTimeS (fixed grips add boarding time), bubble, detachable. Meshes: Chair seats 2/4/6/8 + bubble hood, gondola cabin sizes. `LiftAssist.kindsForThroughput` suggests variants by required riders/h.

**Operations** (`sim/ops/LiftOps.ts`, tuning in `config/liftOps.ts`; ticked from the 'economy' system wrapper in createSim): per lift mode auto/open/closed, opening hours (default 08:30-16:30, wraps midnight), season profiles
(winter / shoulder = spring+autumn / summer; default: bike-capable lifts run in shoulder+summer), eco (half speed with an empty queue), wind closure in storms by tolerance. Closed time costs 15 % upkeep and no energy; running costs energy
per hour (daily settle through Finance `costs().energy`). `l.open` is derived: OpenStatus conditions (`condOpen`) AND not held by ops (`opsClosed/opsReason`); 'hours'/'inspection' holds still count as open for rating/arrivals.
`ops:schedule` (aggregate) fires on a season-profile change or a bulk action.

**Wear & breakdowns**: condition 0-100 falls with operating hours x load x weather, routine maintenance restores a little each day (more with a Lift Maintenance Workshop, building `liftWorkshop`). Inspection none/basic/thorough
(periodic, closes the lift at night, cuts breakdown risk 40-65 %). Breakdown chance per operating hour = f(condition, age, weather, inspection), seeded RNG. A breakdown closes the lift, bills 'maintenance', freezes riders on the cable
(happiness drops), creates a `stranded` incident (Incidents: severity escalates minor->major->critical; patrol/first-aid/heli resolve it = evacuation to the bottom station; fallback crew rope-rescue after 24/36 ticks; extreme cold + long wait counts as a severe evacuation: rating hit, unhappy guests, nobody dies). Repair hours/cost: workshop x0.5 time; contractor x1.8 time, +4 h call-out, x2.5 cost. Events `lift:broken`, `lift:repaired`, `incident:stranded` (news only for stranded).
UI: lift inspector (condition bar, inspection, ETA, Inspect now / Repair / Close, hours, eco, seasons), Operations drawer (`st-operations`: lifts x seasons table, quick actions, projected energy).
