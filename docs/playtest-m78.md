# Playtest M7.8: Schirmbar and the resort name (0.6.0)

Nobody has played the Schirmbar by hand: everything below was checked headless and on the real page with a software renderer (SwiftShader), which cannot judge the look at real frame rates, the sound, or how it feels. Please check on a real machine and a phone.

## Schirmbar
- [ ] Career: the Schirmbar is locked until resort level 2 (Local favourite); Sandbox has it. It is in Services; the icon and card read well.
- [ ] Place one right beside a piste and one right beside a lift station: both accepted, no road or footpath needed. Placing one ON a piste tile or in water is refused with a clear reason.
- [ ] On a clear winter day it is open (card: Open). Skiers coming down stop for a drink now and then (not constantly), the card shows today's guests and income. Does the income feel small enough next to the Apres-Ski Bar?
- [ ] Storm, heavy snow, rain, fog, strong wind: it closes, the card says why, the roof is pulled down and the lights are off. It opens again by itself afterwards.
- [ ] The **Closed** tick closes it in good weather; unticking opens it. Large enough to hit on a phone (44 px).
- [ ] Summer: "Closed for the season", shuttered, no music, no income.
- [ ] Zoom in on an open bar: apres-ski music fades in (the game music dips), and it goes away when the bar closes or you zoom out. With two or three bars it does not get louder without limit.
- [ ] Look: the striped roof and golden cap, glass walls, the red banner with your resort name (readable when zoomed in), seated guests and the glow at dusk. Does it look right beside a lift station (queue space)? Is the roof too white under snow?
- [ ] Rudi suggests one in winter once you have a lift and a crowd.

## Resort name
- [ ] New Game shows a suggested name (changes with the seed until you edit it); the dice gives another; 24 characters at most; the name is on the banner after Start.
- [ ] Settings > Gameplay > Resort name renames it; the banner follows within a second. Empty goes back to the suggestion.
- [ ] Save, quit, load: the name and the Closed tick come back. An old save gets a name from its seed.
- [ ] The name shows in the level tooltip in the HUD, the Year-end report and as the photo file name.

## Numbers to judge
Drinks a day, income, how often skiers stop (about one run in four within three tiles), whether 60 drinks a day is too many or too few, whether wind above 0.3 closes it too often (about one winter day in seven).
