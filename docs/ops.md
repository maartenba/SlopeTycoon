# Operations safety (Task 26)

Modules in `src/sim/ops/` (pure sim; bundled by `Safety`, stepped by the `ops` system after grooming; 1 tick = 6 game min):

- **Incidents** – queue/state machine (`open → responding → treating → transport → resolved`), kinds `injury|buried|attack|stranded`, severity minor/major/critical. Unattended minor→major (60 t)→critical (+90 t)→ a neglected critical case ends as `hospitalised` (severe, 80 t); guests trapped in the snow (`buried` kind, shown as "trapped in the snow") have 15 ticks for a dig-out, after which the hospital transfer is forced (severe); hard timeout 600 t so nothing blocks forever. **Nobody dies**: outcomes are `recovered` (treated on site, returns), `hospitalised` (leaves early), `evacuated` (stranded riders brought down) or `left`. Severe (late) cases carry the heavy penalty and a bigger happiness hit nearby. `stats()` exposes open/unresolved/meanResponseTicks/recovered/hospitalised/evacuated/severe/severeAvoided/escalations/penalty. Old saves with `fatal` outcomes/stats load as severe hospitalisations.
- **Patrol** – sleds per Patrol Hub (staff +/-, 0..6) and a walker per First-Aid Hut (minors on site only). Straight-line travel at 4 tiles/tick (0.55x off-piste), treat, carry to nearest hut/hub. Cost 60 per mission (`rescue`).
- **Heli** – one per active Helipad (range 64), flies to the nearest map edge (off-map hospital), away 4 ticks, returns. 900 per mission. Critical / slow-patrol / out-of-range majors escalate to it.
- **Avalanche** (rarity pass) – rare, dramatic, mostly off-piste in storms. Per-tile instability builds only on tiles with steepness >= 1.5 half-steps/tile (~37 deg; 30-45 deg is worst, steeper ground tapers), snow depth >= 0.5, **tile temperature <= 2 C** (none on warm days / in summer) and **fresh snow >= 2.0** (48 h memory, ~12 h of heavy snowfall). Gain is x1.4 off-piste, x0.5 piste, x0.2 groomed, up to x1.45 with a temperature rise, x2 rain-on-snow, x0.35 under patrol cover (release chance x0.4), forest protection up to -75 %. Release rules: at most 1 uncontrolled release per game day, >= 6 days between two releases anywhere, per-winter soft cap (chance x0.15 per release after 2; a winter ends after 100 release-free days), same ground has a 6-day cooldown. Slide size follows instability: barely unstable ground only **sloughs** (< 12 tiles, no piste/guest hit = cosmetic: no event, closure or news; counted in `sloughs`). Notable slides clear snow, bury guests (critical incident), close pistes (`OpenStatus.closeTiles`) for 8 h (24 h when guests are buried) and emit `incident:avalanche`; the news row is rate-limited to one per 3 days (`quiet` events still drive sound/visuals; buried guests always report). Night control blasting (04:00, Patrol Hub cover, threshold 0.9) releases + stabilises covered slopes and emits one event (one 'Avalanche control' info row) per night. `scripts/analysis/avalancheSurvey.ts [days] [sizes] [seeds]` measures releases on starter resorts (before the rarity pass: 900-1100 uncontrolled/yr on 64, 4300-5600 on 128, 15700-19600 on 256, ~100-150 of them in summer; after: 0-3 per winter on every size, 0 in summer).
- **Wildlife** – bears (territories, hibernate in winter, hungry in autumn) and wolves roam remote forest (`forestDensity>=170`, >=6 tiles from buildings/paths), stalk off-piste skiers / remote hikers; bears also scare guests inside a threat field and cost revenue (see `docs/nature.md` *Bears & wildlife management*); Warning Signs x0.4, patrol coverage x0.5 attack chance; patrol sweeps relocate animals; the **Ranger Station** (coverage `wildlife`) hazes / relocates bears, calms guests and funds bear-proof bins.

Hooks: `GuestSim.injuryHook`, `GuestSim.safetyPort()`, `GState.Injured`, `Decider.offPisteScale/hikePenalty`.
Task 27 rating inputs: `safety.incidents.stats()` (`open`, `unresolved`, `meanResponseTicks`, `severe`, `severeAvoided`, `penalty` 0..1).
Visuals (`IncidentView`) are unverified at runtime (no WebGL).

## Lift schedules follow the sun (update)
New lifts default to **Auto (daylight)**: open sunrise + 45 min .. sunset - 15 min (winter 08:30-16:30, summer 06:00-20:30); `LiftOpsData.daylight` (undefined on old saves = manual hours). Choosing hours manually switches it off. Grooming runs in the dark window (sunset + 45 min .. sunrise - 45 min) instead of 22:00-06:00. Details: `docs/ski-exploration.md`.


## Snowmaking cannons (fix3: "the cannon does one tile")

Cause (measured on the 128 starter resort): a cannon covered a disc of radius 6, but snow was only added to *piste* tiles (a 3-wide ribbon): ~35 piste tiles per cannon, nothing next to the piste, and on an already-snowy piste nothing at all. Now (`sim/ops/Snowmaking.ts`, `SNOWMAKING`):

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| throw radius (`BUILDINGS.snowCannon.serviceRadius`) | 6 | **10** (pipe range from a reservoir stays 24) |
| tiles served per mid-piste cannon (128 starter) | 35 piste, 0 ground | **111 (59 piste + 52 ground within 5 tiles)** |
| output | flat 5 cm/h, piste only | 8 cm/h at the nozzle, fan: -45 % at the edge; ground within 5 tiles at 60 % rate up to 0.3 m; piste up to 0.6 m |
| temperature | <= -2 °C only | <= -2 °C full rate, **-2..0 °C half rate (wet-bulb)**, > 0 °C off |
| water | 1 unit per tile-cm, 20 000 reservoir | 0.25 per tile-cm: 5 cannons build a first 0.6 m base from bare ground for ~1 800 water; a reservoir (20 000 + 300/day streams, rain, melt, 3 000/day in summer) carries 8-12 cannons through a winter |
| a cold night (10 h) on bare ground | n/a | >= 0.3 m on every covered piste tile (tested), 0.45-0.5 m typical |

UI: the inspector of a cannon shows `Running / Idle: too warm (needs <= -2 °C at 1,450 m; 0 °C at half rate; now x °C) / Idle: no water in the reservoir / Not connected to a reservoir: ... / Out of range of piste / Idle: snow base complete`, covered tiles (piste), last night's average gain (cm) and water used, and the reservoir level; a reservoir lists how many cannons it feeds. The **Snowmaking overlay** shows the coverage (cyan) and glows white-blue where snow was actually made last night / tonight (`Snowmaking.gainToday/gainLast`, rolled at noon). Render: `src/render/SnowSpray.ts` = looping fan of 40 billboards per running cannon, animated in the vertex shader, one instanced draw call (<= 12 cannons, nearest to the view centre, 0 above view height 110, <= 480 instances). Advisor rule `snow.gaps` (winter, reservoir present): >= 24 piste tiles within pipe range but outside any cannon's reach -> "Place a cannon beside the bare stretch" with a valid site. Tests: `tests/sim/ops/cannon.test.ts`, `tests/ui-dom/cannonInspector.test.ts`. Screenshots: `.weave/shots/fix3-cannon-{spray,overlay,inspector}.png`.
