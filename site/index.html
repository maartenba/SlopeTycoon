Slope Tycoon: build lifts and pistes, run a ski resort

I wanted to keep running a mountain after the holiday was over, so I built the game for it. You shape a valley, put in lifts and paint pistes, then try to keep a village alive through winter and summer.

You start with a lodge and a bit of money. Build a lift, paint a run down from the top, add somewhere to eat, and watch the guests work out what you meant.

▶ Play nowRead the guide

Free, no download, no account, no ads. It works offline once you have opened it, and it is best on a desktop. It is still a pre-release, so expect rough edges.

{{hero}}

What you actually do

Every new game gives you a different valley. You raise and flatten the ground, lay a road, put a lift at the foot of a slope and paint a piste down the hill: green, blue, red or black. Guests arrive, buy a pass, ride the lift, ski down and tell you what they thought.

Once that loop works, the resort can grow: more lifts, a ski school, a ranger station, snowmaking for the thin months, a hotel on the hill. There are twelve kinds of lift, from a T-bar to a ten-person gondola. How ski lifts work explains them without the game.

The snow line moves with the real calendar, so in April the high slopes still ski while the valley hikes. I wanted summer to matter, so a resort that only works in winter runs out of money by May.

The guide has getting your first lift open, and there is a longer page about playing a ski resort tycoon in your browser.

Lifts, runs and a lot of small details

Every stretch of piste between two junctions is a run with its own name, difficulty and traffic, and guests pick between them. Lifts wear out, wind closes some of them and they all need a yearly service.

You can sit in the chair yourself, walk through the village in first person, or take a photo and save it as a PNG on your device. Ride, walk and photo modes.

Summer is a game of its own

A campground with a campfire, a petting zoo, a viewpoint, quads, a climbing crag, rafting on a river you dug yourself, fishing and a concert on the mountain. I wanted the summer half to be more than the winter game with the snow removed.

Money, goals and a year-end report

Set day and season pass prices, run marketing campaigns, take a loan and watch the interest. Career mode levels your resort up and unlocks bigger lifts, and there are six scenarios if you would rather have a specific problem to solve. Every year ends with a Resort of the Year report. How money works.

Things I like

  • Watching skiers pick a run, and queue where I built one lift too few.
  • Dragging a river from a spring to a lake and seeing it freeze in a hard winter.
  • All of the music and sound is generated by code. There are no audio files.
  • Spring skiing high up while the valley hikes.

Things I’m still fixing

  • Phones show the map now (sheets instead of panels), but I have only tried them on an emulated phone. Tablets are better.
  • Pistes can still be painted over a stream, which they should not.
  • Prices and upkeep get tuned between releases, so the money changes a little each time. The devlog says when.
  • Walk mode needs a mouse the browser can capture. If it misbehaves for you, please tell me on GitHub.
