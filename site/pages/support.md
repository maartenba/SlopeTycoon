I make Slope Tycoon in my spare time. The game is free to play, it has no ads and no accounts, and that does not change if you never give anything.

If you enjoy it and feel like chipping in, you can do that on **Ko-fi**. It works like buying me a coffee: you do not need an account to give, and nothing in the game unlocks because of it.

## What the money does

It pays for the small costs around the game (the domain, tools and testing devices) and gives me more time for new lifts, buildings and fixes. If you give nothing, the game keeps working the same.
