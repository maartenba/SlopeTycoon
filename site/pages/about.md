<p class="story-lead">The story behind Slope Tycoon, in my own words.</p>

<div class="story">

I am Maarten. I like skiing, and every time I came back home from a family ski vacation I wanted the week in the mountains and on the slopes to last longer. Around the year 2000 I started playing [Ski Resort Tycoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ski_Resort_Tycoon) after the vacation to do just that.

And then summer vacations in the mountains, same thing… There was no summer ski town game, I believe, but it's something I would have liked. This is the game I wanted to build, one day. Here we are!

I wanted to experiment with building an application with enough complexity (a game), and see how to structure and build it in an agentic manner. While most of the development was not done by me, the game concept, testing, and thinking were done by myself.

I hope you enjoy the gameplay, graphics, and general concept of the game.

<p class="sign">Maarten</p>

</div>

## How it is built

Slope Tycoon is a browser game made with TypeScript and three.js. Terrain, buildings, textures and icons are generated by code at runtime. Sound effects and ambience mix recorded CC0 samples with sounds made in code, the music is a set of tracks generated with Suno, and the only other files are two fonts. The source is available to read on [GitHub](https://github.com/maartenba/SlopeTycoon); it is not open source: all rights reserved, see the [licence](https://github.com/maartenba/SlopeTycoon/blob/main/LICENSE). It was developed with a lot of help from AI coding agents under my direction.
