## Income and costs

Income comes from **day passes**, **season passes**, restaurants, shops, lodging, rentals and attractions, plus small **grants** for completing goals. Costs are construction, **upkeep**, **wages**, energy for lifts and snow cannons, planned lift **maintenance**, rescues, marketing and loan interest. **Energy** can be cut with the generators in the Buildings drawer's Energy tab (at most 60 % of the lift bill; see [Operations & safety](page:operations)), and switched-off snow cannons cost only 15 % of their upkeep. **Finance** shows the breakdown, monthly reports and the year report.

At the end of each month a small **month summary card** shows income, expenses and net (you can switch it off in [Settings](page:settings)).

## Statistics

{{fig:ui-stats}}

Press <kbd>I</kbd> (or the chart button in the top bar, or the pause menu) to open **Statistics**: charts of **guests**, **cash**, **rating** (with the sustainability facet) and **revenue by category**. Choose **30 days**, **a year**, **two years** or **all time**; older days are kept as monthly summaries, so a long game stays light. Hover a chart, or use the left and right arrow keys, to read exact values; **Show the numbers as a table** lists everything as text. The colours follow your palette setting, so they work for colour-blind players too.

## Pricing

{{fig:ui-pricing}}

Open **Pricing** to set day-pass and season-pass prices, food and shop markups and prices for individual attractions. The game compares each with a **fair price** (which rises with your star rating) and tells you if you are *cheap* or *expensive*. Too cheap, and VIPs get suspicious. Too dear, and budget skiers stay home. Price changes apply immediately, but jumping a price by more than about a third causes a short-lived **price shock** that hurts happiness and arrivals.

You can price by weekday and season too. **Peak days** are real weekends (Saturday and Sunday), holidays and powder days (it is snowing hard); regular days are Fridays; off-peak days are Monday to Thursday. Peak days bring more guests, so a premium is reasonable.

## Marketing campaigns

{{fig:ui-marketing-campaigns}}

Campaigns raise arrivals of certain guests for a while. Each has **prerequisites** that the panel lists (and tells you what to build): a ski school, a kids area, an open lift, an open piste, parking, a restaurant, enough rating, summer attractions and so on. A running campaign pauses if its requirements vanish and is cancelled after a short grace period.

{{gen:campaigns}}

Four campaigns are newer and tell you what to build:

- **Come by Train** needs a **Train Station** on a rail line that reaches the valley, within walking distance of the lodge (or served by a Shuttle Depot). It moves part of the road arrivals onto the train, up to the station's capacity of 150 guests, so the car park empties, and a small, capped bonus for the share arriving by train feeds your sustainability score.
- **Snowboard Park Jam** needs an open **Stunt Park** and brings the freestyle crowd.
- **Hike & Bike Weekends** pulls couples and walkers to your trails, but only on peak days (weekends and holidays): weekdays are weak.
- **Festival Season** needs a booked festival running or starting within a week and works only around the festival dates; outside them it is weak. Book the festival first, then the campaign.

Each card shows a **Show me** button for a missing requirement: it opens the right building card, pans to the station or scrolls to the Events calendar.

Festivals have their own cards just below the campaigns: see [Events, stories & the region](page:events).

## Loans, debt and support

- Take **loans** from the Finance drawer (up to five at once, with a monthly interest rate that depends on difficulty).
- You may go **into debt**: cash can go negative. Interest is charged, the rating suffers a bit, and construction is refused until you are back above zero.
- Staff wages are part of the bill: with the default *Auto* crews there is nothing extra to pay, courses add a small upkeep (see [Operations & safety](page:operations)).
- The first time your cash dips below zero in summer or autumn, the **Tourism Board** offers a one-off interest-free **support loan** for a year.
- On Relaxed and Normal difficulty the bank never forecloses; you decide when to declare bankruptcy. On Challenging, two straight in-game years deep in the red ends the game.

## Difficulty

| Difficulty | Feel |
|---|---|
| Relaxed | More starting money, cheaper upkeep, patient guests, fewer incidents |
| Normal | The intended balance |
| Challenging | Less money, higher upkeep and interest, pickier guests, more incidents |

> [!TIP] Rudi's “cash runway” advice appears when you have under five days of upkeep in the bank. Believe him.
