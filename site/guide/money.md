## Income and costs

Income comes from **day passes**, **season passes**, restaurants, shops, lodging, rentals and attractions, plus small **grants** for completing goals. Costs are construction, **upkeep**, **wages**, energy for lifts, rescues, marketing and loan interest. **Finance** shows the breakdown, monthly reports and the year report.

At the end of each month a small **month summary card** shows income, expenses and net (you can switch it off in [Settings](page:settings)).

## Pricing

{{fig:ui-pricing}}

Open **Pricing** to set day-pass and season-pass prices, food and shop markups and prices for individual attractions. The game compares each with a **fair price** (which rises with your star rating) and tells you if you are *cheap* or *expensive*. Too cheap, and VIPs get suspicious. Too dear, and budget skiers stay home. Price changes apply immediately, but jumping a price by more than about a third causes a short-lived **price shock** that hurts happiness and arrivals.

You can price by weekday and season too. **Peak days** are weekends, holidays and powder days (it is snowing hard); regular days are Fridays; off-peak days are Monday to Thursday. Peak days bring more guests, so a premium is reasonable.

## Marketing campaigns

{{fig:ui-marketing}}

Campaigns raise arrivals of certain guests for a while. Each has **prerequisites** that the panel lists (and tells you what to build): a ski school, a kids area, an open lift, an open piste, parking, a restaurant, enough rating, summer attractions and so on. A running campaign pauses if its requirements vanish and is cancelled after a short grace period.

{{gen:campaigns}}

Festivals have their own cards just below the campaigns: see [Events, stories & the region](page:events).

## Loans, debt and support

- Take **loans** from the Finance drawer (up to five at once, with a monthly interest rate that depends on difficulty).
- You may go **into debt**: cash can go negative. Interest is charged, the rating suffers a bit, and construction is refused until you are back above zero.
- Staff wages are part of the bill: with the default *Auto* crews there is nothing extra to pay, courses add a small upkeep (see [Operations & safety](page:operations)).
- The first time your cash dips below zero in summer or autumn, the **Tourism Board** offers a one-off interest-free **support loan** for a year.
- On Relaxed and Normal difficulty the bank never forecloses; you decide when to declare bankruptcy. On Challenging, two straight in-game years deep in the red ends the game.

## Difficulty

| Difficulty | Feel |
|---|---|
| Relaxed | More starting money, cheaper upkeep, patient guests, fewer incidents |
| Normal | The intended balance |
| Challenging | Less money, higher upkeep and interest, pickier guests, more incidents |

> [!TIP] Rudi's “cash runway” advice appears when you have under five days of upkeep in the bank. Believe him.
