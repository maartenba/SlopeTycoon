Not everybody wants to ski. Three **winter-only** attractions give couples, families, seniors and the adventurous something to do off the slope. All three are in the **Recreation** tab of the Buildings drawer and work like the summer [Quad Base](page:summer-adventures): a base building plus a **route** that the game finds for you, a timetable, a ticket price you can change, and departures that leave on their own.

{{fig:winter-sleigh}}

## Sleigh Stable (level 2)

A **Sleigh Stable** ($5,500, $12 a day, one staff member) sends a **horse-drawn sleigh with six seats** out and back along the **roads and footpaths** that touch it. The ticket is **$25**, a ride takes about an hour and a half and leaves every two hours from 10:00 to 16:00 (with at least two riders on board). The horse wears a collar of bells, and when the camera is close you hear them, very quietly. Couples, families and seniors like it best. It is quiet and costs nothing in sustainability.

## Snowshoe Hut (level 2)

{{fig:winter-snowshoe}}

A **Snowshoe Hut** ($2,800, $10 a day) is a small log hut. A guide leads **eight walkers** (big flat snowshoes, packs, two poles each) along a **Hiking trail** that starts at the hut. Paint the trail first (Pistes & Trails drawer); a plain footpath beside the hut gives the hut access but no route. The ticket is **$15**, a walk takes about two hours and leaves every three hours from 10:00 to 15:00. It suits solo guests, budget travellers and the adventurous, and it is the cheapest tour in the game.

The trail stays painted all winter. The guide is careful: the walk is **cancelled in a storm** and when the snow on the trail is **unstable** (see [Backcountry & avalanche danger](page:backcountry)); it reopens when the snow settles.

## Snowscooter Base (level 3)

{{fig:winter-scooter}}

A **Snowscooter Base** ($9,000, $30 a day, two staff) sends a **convoy of six snowscooters**, one rider each, along attached **roads, footpaths or hiking trails**, never over a piste. The ticket is **$45**; adventurous guests, VIPs and couples love it. A convoy throws up puffs of snow spray as it goes.

Motors have a price. Each departure **scares wildlife** within a few tiles of the route, and the engine noise costs a little [sustainability](page:events). The penalty is capped at three points in total, shared with the summer Quad Base. The sleigh and the snowshoe walk are completely quiet.

## Good to know

- All three are **closed outside winter** ("Closed for the season") and cancel departures in a **storm** or when the snow on the route is dangerous. Rides already out finish; the queue goes home.
- If you remove a road or trail tile the tour closes with **"Needs a connected route of a-b tiles starting at the base"**: rebuild the connection.
- Raise or lower the ticket price in the building's inspector. Wages and upkeep are small, so a base that is always full pays for itself in a season.
- Rudi suggests them with *Winter idea: off-piste fun* once you have 30 or more guests, a lodge and some road.

> [!TIP] Put the Sleigh Stable on the road you already have, and the Snowshoe Hut where a Hiking trail can run through the forest: the route is found from what is already there.
