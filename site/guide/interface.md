## Top left: menu and tools

- **Undo / redo arrows**: step back and forward through your last ten build actions (also <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd>).
- **Menu** returns to the main menu (it autosaves first).
- **Walk** (the little person) and **Camera** start [walk mode and photo mode](page:modes).
- **Graphics** switches the quality level, useful on a modest laptop.
- **Statistics** (the chart button, or <kbd>I</kbd>): charts of your resort over time, see [Money](page:money).
- **Save**, **Settings** (the gear), **Help** (?) and, when your browser offers it, **Install**.

## Top right: your resort at a glance

- **Money**, **star rating**, guest **happiness** face and the number of guests on the mountain.
- **Date chip**: today's real date, season and year (*Sat 14 Feb · Winter · Year 1*; hover for the day of the year and the time), with **weather, temperature and altitude** of the current view and how long until the next season.
- **Speed controls**: pause, 1×, 3× and 10×, with today's date under them. The moon button speeds up nights (a small badge shows when it is on), and the skip button next to the speeds is **Skip to…** (next month, next season or next event). More in [Guests & seasons](page:guests-seasons).
- **Goals card** with the three active goals and their rewards. A badge shows your Career level.

## The dock (bottom)

Each button opens a **drawer** with items or panels. Click again, or press <kbd>Esc</kbd>, to close it.

| Button | What it holds |
|---|---|
| Buildings | Everything you can build, in tabs by type |
| Lifts | The eleven lift types |
| Paths & Roads | Footpaths, roads and rail, with the **Crossings** choice (level crossing or bridge over rail; bridges over water are automatic) |
| Pistes & Trails | Ski pistes by difficulty, plus hiking and biking trails |
| Terrain | Raise, lower, flatten, smooth the ground (sculpting only) |
| Landscape | Dig ponds, boulders, plant or clear trees, garden decor (see [Building](page:building)) |
| Bulldoze | Remove buildings, lifts, paths and pistes |
| View | Camera (rotate, zoom), modes (ride-along, walk, photo), map overlays and season preview |
| Finance | Money overview, loans, reports |
| Operations | Lifts and the **Staff** tab (crews, wages, six training courses), grooming, snowmaking and safety coverage |
| Marketing | Campaigns |
| Pricing | Ticket, season-pass and food prices |

{{fig:ui-view-drawer}}

The **View** drawer groups **Camera** (rotate, zoom), **Modes** (ride-along, walk, photo), **Overlays** and **Season** as cards with key badges; every shortcut still works without opening it.

## Bottom left and right

- **Advice** (bottom left, next to Rudi's face) shows up to three suggestions. **Show me** moves the camera (or opens the right drawer and highlights the card); **Dismiss** hides one for 30 in-game days.
- **Advice** shows Rudi's full message: the card grows to a maximum height and the list scrolls, instead of cutting text with an ellipsis. Goal names in the Goals card wrap too.
- **News** (bottom right) collects events, milestones and guest reviews. Important ones pop up as toasts.

## Update card, What's new and About

When a new version is waiting, a small card at the top left (nothing is paused) lists a few highlights, with **Reload now** (autosaves first) and **Later** (a picture is in [Saves, offline & install](page:saves)).

The first time you launch a new version you get a one-time **What's new** card, and the **About** dialog (Menu) has a *What's new* button and a discreet **Support the game** link to GitHub Sponsors. It is a plain link that opens only when you click it; the game stays free, with no ads and no nagging.

## Help, pause and the camera

{{fig:ui-help}}

Press <kbd>?</kbd> or <kbd>H</kbd> for the shortcut list. When nothing else is open, <kbd>Esc</kbd> opens the pause menu.

{{fig:ui-pause}}

Camera basics: <kbd>W</kbd><kbd>A</kbd><kbd>S</kbd><kbd>D</kbd> or the arrow keys pan, <kbd>Q</kbd> and <kbd>E</kbd> rotate in 90° steps, the mouse wheel zooms, right-drag pans and Alt-drag rotates freely. A full list is in [Keyboard shortcuts](page:shortcuts).

> [!NOTE] On a tablet or phone the same screen adapts: see [Touch & tablets](page:touch).

> [!TIP] Click any building, lift, piste or guest to open its **inspector**: status, staff, prices, and why something is not working.
