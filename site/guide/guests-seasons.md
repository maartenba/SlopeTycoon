## Your guests

{{fig:slope-guests}}

Guests come as **solo** travellers, **couples**, **families**, **budget** skiers, **VIPs** and **adventurers**. Each type has its own taste in prices, its own patience and its own favourite things to do. In winter they ski or snowboard (roughly one in three boards), in summer they hike or ride mountain bikes, and some just relax at the lake or the rink.

Guests have needs: they queue for lifts, get hungry, tire, get cold and sometimes get hurt. When they leave they judge the resort, and the average becomes your **star rating**.

### Reviews

The **News** panel shows guest reviews in their own words: quiet slopes, long gondola queues, great hot chocolate. Treat them as free consulting.

### Click a guest

Click any guest to open the **guest inspector**: type, mood, what they are doing and a short bio. From there you can **Ride along** (see [Ride, Walk & Photo](page:modes)). A few guests carry a name and a wish; see [Events, stories & the region](page:events).

## The year

A game year has **180 days** in 12 months of 15 days:

| Season | Length | What it means for you |
|---|---|---|
| Spring | 30 days | The snowline climbs. High lifts still ski, the valley turns green. |
| Summer | 60 days | Hiking, biking, lakes and farms. Lifts that carry bikes shine. |
| Autumn | 30 days | A breathing space: maintenance, bears get hungry, the first snow. |
| Winter | 60 days | Peak ski season: queues, grooming nights, holidays. |

### The real snowline

Snow depends on **altitude and temperature**. Low pistes melt out first, high ones last, which is why **spring skiing** works only up high while the valley already hikes. Buildings show a *closed: too warm at 1,240 m* message when the air is wrong for them. **Snowmaking** buys you time on a thin base (see [Lifts & pistes](page:lifts-pistes)).

### Summer is not a holiday

Winter buildings are mothballed in summer and cost a fraction of their upkeep, but you still pay wages, loans and the rest. Build hiking and biking trails, an Adventure Park, the beach or boat rental, and run a summer campaign. Rudi warns you in late spring to save a buffer.

## Day and night

Guests ski between morning and the late afternoon. At night windows glow, lamp posts light and **groomers** go to work. The moon button on the speed controls makes nights pass about three times faster.

{{fig:night-village}}

## Time controls

One in-game day lasts **150 seconds** at normal speed (1×). The speed buttons:

| Button | Day lasts about |
|---|---|
| Pause (<kbd>Space</kbd>) | stopped (you can still build) |
| 1× | 150 s |
| 3× | 50 s |
| 10× | 15 s |

Use <kbd>+</kbd> / <kbd>.</kbd> for faster and <kbd>-</kbd> / <kbd>,</kbd> for slower.

### Skip to…

The skip button (next to the speed controls), or <kbd>K</kbd>, fast-forwards: **Next month**, **Next season** or **Next event**. It stops by itself when something important happens, and any click, <kbd>Esc</kbd> or <kbd>Space</kbd> cancels it. It is not available in ride-along, walk or photo mode, or in the tutorial.

> [!TIP] Waiting for money to build the next gondola? Skip a month, then read the monthly summary.
