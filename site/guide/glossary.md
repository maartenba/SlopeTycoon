**Advice** The suggestions from Ranger Rudi at the bottom left. See [Career & scenarios](page:career).

**Almabtrieb** The autumn descent of the decorated cows from the alpine pastures; a festival you can watch.

**Annual service** The yearly planned maintenance of a lift (Operations → Maintenance); a missed one makes the lift *overdue*.

**Auto-flatten** Automatic levelling of the ground under a building when you place it (<kbd>F</kbd> toggles).

**Career** The default game mode where your resort levels up and unlocks big items. Opposite of *Sandbox*.

**Clearance** Free air under a lift cable above trees, roofs and other lifts.

**Comfort** The need for a toilet: an urge that grows while guests are out; it feeds the Facilities bar of the rating.

**Crew** One of the four staff groups: instructors, patrol, groomers and lift operators.

**Date chip** The date, season and year shown in the top bar.

**Dock** The row of buttons along the bottom of the screen.

**Drawer** A panel that opens from a dock button.

**Energy grid** The generators (solar, wind, hydro, biomass) that offset a capped share of your lift energy bill.

**Eco Centre** A level-4 building that opens the *Eco programme*: five paid policies and a carbon-neutral certificate that lift the Sustainability score by up to 29 points.

**Events strip** The January to December overview in the Events calendar.

**Facilities** The rating bar (6 %) for toilets: comfort satisfaction, coverage of pistes and village, blocks per guest.

**Fair price** The price the game considers reasonable for a ticket at your star rating.

**Fast nights** An option that makes nights pass faster.

**Footbridge** A raised timber crossing for footpaths: over a rail line (no waiting for trains) or over a stream, pond or gap. Bridges are built as part of drawing the path; there is no separate tool.

**Green Resort** A year-report award for a resort that is gentle on forest, wildlife and energy.

**Grooming** Overnight smoothing of piste snow by groomers.

**Incident** A guest injury, burial, stranding or animal encounter.

**Inspector** The panel that opens when you click a building, lift, piste or guest.

**Level** Career progress from 1 to 6.

**Level crossing** A footpath or road crossing a rail line on the level; barriers close for trains and guests wait.

**Mothballed** A snow cannon that is switched off: no energy, no water, 15 % of its upkeep.

**Not connected** A building that has no path, road or rail link yet.

**Overlay** A map view (coverage, snowmaking, wildlife…); <kbd>O</kbd> cycles them.

**Piste** A marked ski run. Green, blue, red and black by difficulty.

**Profile** Your local trophies, cosmetics and medals, separate from saves.

**Profile card** The small side view of altitude and difficulty shown while you draw a piste or lift.

**Ranger Rudi** The friendly adviser at the bottom left.

**Run** One stretch of piste between two junctions or lift stations, with its own name, difficulty, traffic and open / closed switch.

**Runs overlay** The map view that colours and labels every run.

**Sandbox** Everything unlocked from day one.

**Service window** The month in which a lift gets its annual service.

**Skip to…** Fast-forward to the next month, season or event (<kbd>K</kbd>).

**Snowline** The altitude above which snow lies at a given time of year.

**Snowmaking** Cannons fed from a reservoir that make artificial snow in the cold.

**Spring skiing** Skiing on high pistes in spring while the valley is already green.

**Story guest** A guest with a name and one wish.

**Tile** One square of the map (about 10 m).

**Toilets** A free-to-use hut that eases the comfort need; stands on a footpath, a trail or a piste edge.
