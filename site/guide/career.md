## Career or Sandbox?

**Career** (the default for new games) gives your resort a **level** from 1 to 6. A short list of big items unlocks as you level up. **Sandbox** unlocks everything from the start and tracks no level. The goal chain works in both.

## Levels

{{fig:ui-level-panel}}

A level is reached when **all** of its requirements hold. Levels are *latched*: once reached, never lost, even after a bad winter.

### What unlocks when

Everything not listed is available from the start (including the starter chain of lifts, lodge, ticket office, parking, ski school, patrol hub, first-aid hut and the ranger station).

## Goals

The goals card walks you through **four chapters and twenty goals**: *Season 1: Open for business*, *Local favourite*, *Regional name* and *Alpine destination*. Three goals are active at a time; finishing a chapter opens the next. Each goal pays a small grant. Click **Show me** to jump the camera to the relevant spot, and the card can be collapsed.

## Ranger Rudi's advice

Rudi is the friendly ranger in the bottom-left corner. Every in-game hour he checks the state of your resort, such as long queues, missing patrol, unused lifts, an icy piste, a bear without a ranger station, low cash and a price far from fair, and shows up to three pieces of advice, most urgent first. **Show me** points at the place; **Dismiss** snoozes an item for 30 days. Advice goes away as soon as the problem does.

## Scenarios

{{fig:ui-scenarios}}

Scenarios are handcrafted challenges with a fixed map, season, starting situation and objectives. Reach the required objectives to win; bonus objectives raise your score and earn a **medal** (bronze, silver or gold). Pick them under **Scenarios** on the main menu or in the New Game dialog.

{{gen:scenarios}}

## Resort of the Year

At the end of every in-game year (180 days) the game shows a **report card**: guests, income, rating, best moments and awards, with charts. It pauses the game while open and you can switch it off in [Settings](page:settings); past reports stay under **Finance → Reports**. The report can be saved as a PNG on your device. Nothing is ever uploaded.
