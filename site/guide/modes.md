These three modes put the camera at ground level. They are **read-only**: they never change your resort, and when you leave them the camera returns exactly to where it was.

## Ride along

{{fig:ride-ski}}

Click a guest, then **Ride along** in the inspector. You see the world from their eyes: skiing down a piste, gliding up a lift or walking to the lodge.

- <kbd>N</kbd> hops to the next guest, <kbd>C</kbd> recentres your view.
- Drag to look around, wheel to change the field of view.
- <kbd>Esc</kbd> exits. Speed keys (<kbd>Space</kbd>, <kbd>+</kbd>, <kbd>-</kbd>) still work.
- By default the game runs in **slow motion** while you ride, otherwise a run would be over in a second. You can switch this off in Settings.

## Walk mode

{{fig:walk-lodge}}

Press <kbd>V</kbd> and you are standing on the nearest footpath. Walk with <kbd>W</kbd><kbd>A</kbd><kbd>S</kbd><kbd>D</kbd>, hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> to run, press <kbd>Space</kbd> to hop.

- **Look** by holding the left mouse button and dragging (the cursor is never captured). If you prefer, Settings → *Walk: mouse look* can switch to **pointer lock**, where hold <kbd>Alt</kbd> frees the mouse.
- <kbd>E</kbd> **interacts**: inspect the building or guest in front of you, or board a lift at its bottom station for a scenic ride (you are not a guest, so nobody charges you or queues).
- <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>V</kbd> lets you **choose the start**: a figure follows your cursor and a ring shows whether the tile is walkable (green) or blocked (red: you will be dropped at the nearest free spot). You can also drag the little walker button onto the map.
- <kbd>T</kbd> teleports: back to the bird's-eye view to choose again.
- <kbd>V</kbd> or <kbd>Esc</kbd> leaves walk mode.

## Photo mode

{{fig:photo-mode}}

Press <kbd>P</kbd> (or the camera button) from the normal view, a ride-along or a walk. Orbit with the mouse, move the focus with <kbd>W</kbd><kbd>A</kbd><kbd>S</kbd><kbd>D</kbd>, raise and lower it with <kbd>Q</kbd>/<kbd>E</kbd>. The panel offers **filters**, **frames** (some are unlocked by [trophies](page:achievements)), aspect ratio, vignette, field of view and the **time of day**, so you can fake a golden hour.

Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to **save the photo**: it downloads as a PNG to your device. Nothing is uploaded. <kbd>P</kbd> or <kbd>Esc</kbd> exits.

> [!TIP] The camera can be taken at 2× scale for a bigger picture. A “hero” preset picks a nice angle for you.
