Summer is the second half of the year, and it pays if you give people something to do. **Hikers and bikers** use the trails and the lifts that carry bikes; the buildings below give them somewhere to sleep, play, climb, paddle and fish. All of them sit in the **Recreation** tab of the Buildings drawer (the campground is listed there too) and wear a small summer tag. In Career each has a resort level: the *Campground*, *Petting Zoo* and *Viewpoint* are open from level 1, the *Tackle & Bait Hut*, *Treehouse*, *Quad Base* and *Climbing Crag* from level 2, and the *Rafting* and *Canyoning* bases from level 3 (the full table is in [Career & scenarios](page:career)).

## What to build

| Building | Cost | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Campground** | $3,500 | 16 pitches around a campfire, $24 a night, summer only. Needs a footpath or hiking trail. Budget and adventure guests pitch their tent first, and the evening fire cheers campers up (more with a toilet within 10 tiles). At night the fire glows and smokes. |
| **Petting Zoo** | $3,000 | A fenced pen with goats, sheep, rabbits and a donkey. $8 entry, 20 places, a keeper, closed in snow. Families love it. No farm needed. |
| **Viewpoint** | $1,500 | A timber platform with a telescope. Hikers walk to it for the view, stop longer and cheer up; it is also a spot in Photo mode. Build it on **high ground** beside a trail, a footpath or a piste edge: a low viewpoint hardly attracts anyone. |
| **Treehouse** | $2,500 | A house in the trees with a rope bridge. It needs a forest (at least 6 trees within 3 tiles), keeps the trees and works like a viewpoint. |
| **Quad Base** | $6,500 | A convoy of four quads (8 riders, $40 each) rides out and back along a road or footpath from 10:00 to 16:00 every two hours. Closed in wind and snow. The engines scare wildlife and cost a little sustainability (never more than 3 points). |
| **Climbing Crag** | $4,500 | A hut at the foot of a real rock face: it needs steep bare rock within three tiles and a trail or footpath. Eight climbers at $30 each. An instructor is optional: without one the crag runs at half capacity, with more twisted ankles (the Ski Patrol answers them). |
| **Rafting Base** | $5,500 | Two four-person rafts go downstream from 10:00 to 16:00, $30 a ride. Needs a river or stream with brisk flow within three tiles. |
| **Canyoning Base** | $4,800 | Abseiling descents of a steep stream through a rocky gorge, $35. Needs a steep stretch and a gorge, at any flow. |
| **Tackle & Bait Hut** | $1,800 | Day tickets, rod hire and bait. See [Quads, climbing, rafting & fishing](page:summer-adventures). |
| **Fishing Pier** | $400 | Four fishing places on a lake or pond; needs a hut first. |

{{fig:summer-petting-zoo}}

**Viewpoint and Treehouse** are for hikers. Place the viewpoint on high ground beside a trail (the higher, the better: hikers weigh the view against the summit pull), and the treehouse inside a forest. Both are also spots in Photo mode (the *Viewpoint* button), and a viewpoint on high ground pays a bonus goal.

{{fig:summer-treehouse}}

> [!TIP] Click a building after placing it: the inspector says why something is closed (*Closed for the season*, *no instructor*, *Needs a river with brisk flow*) and the placement ghost explains why a spot is refused.

## Two summer campaigns

Both are in the Marketing drawer (see [Money](page:money)):

- **Family Summer Camp** ($6,000, 14 days) needs a Campground plus a kids area or a Petting Zoo. It brings families, a little more with a Petting Zoo, and does not need a Ski School.
- **Angler's Paradise** ($3,500, 14 days) needs a Tackle & Bait Hut on water that still has fish. It brings anglers (and a few couples), works in summer and autumn and at half strength on winter ice, and it fades when the fish run low.

## A summer plan

1. Keep a bike-capable lift (any chair or gondola) open and paint a hiking and an MTB trail along it: **Mountain bikers and hikers** are your base crowd.
2. Add the cheap, wide-appeal buildings first: a **Campground** with toilets close to it, a **Petting Zoo** and a **Viewpoint** on the ridge.
3. Run **Hike & Bike Weekends** or the **summer** campaign, then **Family Summer Camp** once the campground stands.
4. From level 2 add a Tackle & Bait Hut and a Quad Base, from level 3 a rafting or canyoning base on a river: see [Quads, climbing, rafting & fishing](page:summer-adventures).
5. Keep a cash buffer: Ranger Rudi warns you in late spring. In the bot's year-two check a resort with these buildings earns about a fifth to a quarter of its yearly income in summer.
