Two more guest types fill the quiet corners of the calendar: **Seniors** and **School groups**. Both are unlocked from **resort level 2** and need little more than the things a normal resort already has.

## Seniors

Slow, comfortable and well-off (lilac cardigans, grey or white hair). They come **singly or as a pair**, mostly **on weekdays** (a weekday is 1.4 times as likely as the baseline, a weekend 0.7) and in the **quiet seasons**, with a spend of $350 to $650 and a good chance of staying the night.

- They **tire faster** and need a **toilet more often** (twice as fast), so they want somewhere to sit and somewhere to go. They only come to a resort with at least one **Heated Terrace, Café or Toilets**.
- They **never ride black runs**: their skill stops at intermediate (red).
- They love a **terrace or café**: a meal goes to a Heated Terrace or a Café when one is open, and they stop for a coffee even without being hungry. They also like a **view**, the **pool** of a Luxury Hotel, sleigh and guided tours and the petting zoo, and they go hiking only about a quarter of the time.

## School groups

A class arrives **by coach** (or train): **one teacher and 8 to 15 children**, all in **orange bibs**, with a small budget ($70 to $130 each) and no overnight stay.

- **Weekdays only, never on a public holiday.** In winter they come in the **first week of December** (1 to 8 December) and in **January**; in summer on any weekday.
- **They need something for the kids**: in winter a **Ski School HQ, Kids Ground or Magic Carpet**; in summer a **Playground, Petting Zoo or Campground**.
- The teacher walks the class to a kids venue; a big class is taught in **batches of six** so a ski school with two instructors can cope. They eat together and go home together.
- They share the guest limit of the resort like any other guest: a full resort turns a coach away.

## Campaigns

{{fig:ui-marketing-campaigns}}

- **Wellness & Views** ($8,000, 21 days; summer, autumn and winter, half strength in spring) aims at **Seniors** (+160 %) and **couples** (+60 %), and needs **a hotel pool or a viewpoint, a restaurant, café or terrace, and toilets**. It asks for **3 stars** and a premium price level.
- **School Trips** ($4,000, 14 days; winter and summer) aims at **School groups** (+200 %) and needs a **ski school, a kids area and cheap lodging**; it only works on weekdays (Monday to Thursday strongest, weekends and holidays barely). It gets 15 % better while **Come by Train** runs.
- **Come by Train** and **Family Summer Camp** now also reach Seniors and School groups (a smaller push than the two campaigns above).

Rudi will suggest both: *Seniors and couples would love a Wellness & Views campaign* and *Quiet weekdays: try the School Trips campaign* (in January or summer). See [Money](page:money) for how campaigns work in general.
