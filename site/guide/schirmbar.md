The **Schirmbar** is a small domed bar with a striped yellow and white roof, glass walls and a red banner around the bottom. It sits on the slope itself: skiers coming down a piste stop for a quick drink, then carry on. It is in the **Services** tab of the Buildings drawer and unlocks at **resort level 2** (*Local favourite*) in Career; Sandbox has it from the start.

- **Cost**: $1,500, $5 a day, one staff member. A drink is **$6** (the price slider in its card goes up to $14).
- **Size**: 2 x 2 tiles.
- **Where**: anywhere on open ground, **right next to a piste or a lift station** if you like. It cannot stand *on* a piste tile or in water. It needs **no road, no footpath and no power**: skiers ski up to it.
- **Open**: winter only, 10:00 to 17:30.

## When it opens

A Schirmbar opens on clear and cloudy winter days, and in light snow. It closes, and its card says why, when:

- it is a **storm**, **heavy snow** or **rain** ("Closed: storm", "Closed: heavy snow", "Closed: rain");
- the wind is high ("Closed: high wind") or the fog is thick ("Closed: fog");
- it is not winter ("Closed for the season").

A closed bar has its roof pulled down like a tarpaulin, the lights are off and nobody sits inside. When the weather clears it opens again by itself.

You can also close it yourself: tick **Closed** in its card ("Closed by you"). It stays shut, in any weather, until you untick it. Upkeep is paid either way.

## What it does for the resort

- Skiers who pass within about three tiles of an open bar stop on about one run in four, buy a drink, feel a little better and a little less tired, and carry on. Nobody stops twice on the same run.
- It earns small money: at most **60 drinks a day**, then the glasses are out and guests are turned away. The card shows today's guests and income.
- It plays **apres-ski music** for anyone nearby. Like the Apres-Ski Bar, you only hear it while the bar is open, in daytime hours, and when you are zoomed in close.
- It is no replacement for the Lodge or the Cafe: a drink takes only a little off the hunger.

> [!TIP] Put it where a piste bends down toward a lift station. That is where skiers pass closest, and where a queue is worth a drink.

## Your resort's name

Every resort now has a name. The **New Game** dialog suggests one made from your seed (*Alpenglow*, *Val Clair*, *Pine Ridge* and so on). Type your own, or press the dice for another. You can change it later in **Settings > Gameplay > Resort name**.

The name is plain text of **24 characters at most**. It is saved with the game and shows up on the Schirmbar banner, in the level tooltip at the top, in the Year-end report and as the file name of photos from Photo mode.
