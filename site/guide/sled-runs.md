Skiing is not for everybody. A **sled run** (a *Rodelbahn*) is a narrow toboggan track that families, couples, seniors and school classes enjoy, and the **alpine coaster** is its summer cousin on a steel rail. Both work like the other [winter tours](page:winter-adventures): a base building, a ticket you can price, and departures that leave on their own. The difference is that **you paint the track yourself**.

## Sled Rental and the Sled run (level 2)
1. Build a **Sled Rental** (Recreation tab, $4,200, $14 a day, one staff member) at the foot of a gentle slope, beside a footpath or road.
2. Open the **Pistes** drawer, pick **Sled run** ($22 a tile) and drag a track from the hut up the slope. The stroke must start next to the hut, a path, a lift station or an existing run. It cannot cross a piste or a trail (footpaths and roads may cross it) and it cannot cross water.
3. **Gentle slopes only.** A tile steeper than a red piste is refused (*Too steep for sleds*). A run that is almost flat is built but the hut says *Too flat: sleds stop*. Aim for a steady, moderate fall of 10 to 40 tiles.

The hut runs **in winter only** (it needs snow) from 10:00 to 16:00, with a departure every hour and eight sleds per departure. The ticket is **$12**. It closes in a storm and when the snow is unstable, and there are **no floodlights**, so it never runs at night. Rides are fun, never dangerous: a very rare tumble is a minor injury the Ski Patrol looks after.

### A lift that carries sleds
Gondolas, cable cars and **detachable chairlifts** carry sleds. When an **open** one has a station within three tiles of **both ends** of the run, nobody walks back up and the ride is **half as long**, so the same hut earns about twice as much per hour. Without a sled lift the ride includes the walk up. The hut's inspector tells you which case you have.

## Who rides
The new **Sledder** guest type (bright orange jackets, bobble hats; level 2) arrives in winter while a Sled Rental is open, and in summer while an Alpine Coaster is open. But everybody can ride: families, couples, budget guests and seniors like it a lot, school groups too, and skiers and boarders sometimes try it between runs. See [Guests & seasons](page:guests-seasons).

## Alpine Coaster (level 3)
An **Alpine Coaster** station ($38,000, $60 a day, two staff) works the same way in **summer**: pick **Alpine coaster** ($85 a tile) in the Pistes drawer and lay the rail from the station down a slope. The brakes let it take **steeper ground** than a sled run, but not a cliff. Two-seat cars leave every hour from 10:00 to 17:00 for **$18** and run from one to twelve guests. It closes in strong wind. Adventure guests, families and mountain bikers love it.

> [!TIP] Rudi suggests a sled run in winter once you have 40 guests and a base lodge. Put the hut next to the lift that will carry the sleds, and run the track back to the same lift.
