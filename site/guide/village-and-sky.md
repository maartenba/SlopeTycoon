Small buildings that make the village feel lived in, and a few that look up.

## Pizzeria and Bakery (Services, always open)

A **Pizzeria** ($4,800, 2 x 2, beside a road or a footpath) is a real meal at **$9** for 20 guests at a time, open into the evening, with a wood oven that smokes on cold days. Families and budget guests love it. It is cheaper and smaller than the Lodge, so it adds to your dining instead of replacing it.

A **Bakery** ($2,200, 1 x 2, footpath or road) sells rolls and coffee from **06:00 to 12:00** for $4: a quick breakfast, takes a bit off the hunger like the Coffee Stand. Breakfast for the lodgings nearby: a Bakery within 9 tiles of a chalet, apartment, hotel or the Base Lodge adds a small bonus (+4 points, once) to the **Facilities** part of your rating.

## Tourist Office (level 2)

One **Tourist Office** ($3,500, 2 x 2, road or footpath) per resort. While it is open it brings **+6 % arrivals**, always, no matter the season. Rudi suggests it once the resort is settled.

## Skydiving: Grass Strip and Skydive Centre (level 3)

You do **not** need the Altiport. A **Grass Strip** ($6,000, 7 x 2, flat ground beside a road or footpath, one per resort) is a mown strip with a windsock; a small plane takes off from it. A **Skydive Centre** ($7,000, 3 x 2) beside it sells tandem jumps at **$95**: adventure seekers and VIPs book them, families hardly. The plane climbs in a circle, jumpers leave it and the canopies spiral down to the strip.

## Hang gliding and paragliding: Launch Ramp and Landing Field (level 2)

A **Launch Ramp** ($3,500, 2 x 2) on **high ground** beside a trail, a footpath or a piste, plus a **Landing Field** ($1,500, 4 x 4, flat, in the valley) within **45 tiles** and at least 3 units lower. Flights cost **$45**; at most six gliders are drawn at a time.

Everything in the sky flies in **daylight (09:00 to 17:00) and clear weather only**: rain, snow, storms, thick fog and strong wind close it, and the card says why. Nobody gets hurt up there.
