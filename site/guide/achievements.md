Trophies are small milestones: your first guest, your first lift, a month in the black, a good photo and so on. Open the **Trophies** shelf from the main menu. Some are secret and show as ??? until you earn them.

Trophies belong to your **profile** (stored locally in your browser), not to one save game. Rare trophies are worth more points. Some trophies, marked below, give you **cosmetic rewards**: purely visual changes such as building palettes, village flags, photo frames and chair colours. They never change how the game plays.

Use **Settings** to equip a cosmetic; it applies at once without a restart.

> [!TIP] Career-only and scenario-only trophies exist too. A few are tied to specific scenarios.
