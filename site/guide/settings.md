Open **Settings** with the gear icon in the top bar. The game pauses while Settings (and Help, Save & load, Trophies or About) is open.

## Sound

- **Music** and **mute all** (<kbd>M</kbd>), plus volume sliders for master, music, effects and ambience. The music is a set of tracks generated with Suno and provided by the project owner; sound effects and ambience mix recorded CC0 samples, mostly from Freesound, plus one clip of unknown origin (footsteps, birds, bells, wind, rain, water, lifts) with sounds made in code. The first time a recording is needed it is downloaded and kept for offline play, and until then the game plays its own version of the sound. Add `?nosamples=1` to the address to hear only the generated versions.
- **What plays when**: the game picks a track that fits the season, the time of day, the weather and whether a festival is on (a morning pair and a busy winter day, snow, dusk, a calm night and a chill night track, summer, autumn, a storm, a festival). Tracks alternate and never repeat straight away, and the music only changes when a track ends, except for a storm, nightfall or a festival, which fade in a new track. **Next track** skips to another one and shows what is playing; the music keeps playing for a short while even though the game is paused in Settings, so you can preview it. The first time a track plays it is downloaded and kept for offline play.
- **Ambience** (wind, birds, water, crowds, lifts) is quiet by default (28 %) and made of soft noise, with no whistling tones; raise the slider if you want more of it. Some of it depends on where the camera is: birds and other nature sounds are quiet and come forward when you zoom in, streams and rivers babble (or rush over rapids) when you zoom in beside them and go quiet under winter ice, and an open Apres-Ski Bar or Schirmbar plays apres-ski songs when you zoom in close to it, and the game music pauses while you listen and picks up again where it stopped when you zoom back out. All of it follows the ambience volume and mute.
- **Ambience only** and **Reduce loud sounds** for a calmer mix.
- **Pause music at speed 0** if you like silence while paused.

## Look and feel

- **Quality**: *Low*, *Medium* or *High* (also in the top bar as *Graphics*). Lower it on a modest laptop or a phone. **High** also casts real sun shadows from trees, buildings, lifts, guests and vehicles onto the ground; they fade at dusk, vanish at night and are softer in bad weather. Soft contact shadows under buildings and forests are on at every setting. The game never starts on High by itself: try it, and go back to Medium if the map stutters.
- **Atmosphere**: *Off*, *Low* or *High* for valley fog, special sunrises, stars and the moon, mist, frost and rainbows. Off looks exactly like 0.3.1; the default is High on a desktop and Low on touch screens. See [Atmosphere](page:atmosphere).
- **Feedback intensity**: *Off*, *Subtle* or *Lively* controls popups, puffs, confetti and festival fireworks when things happen.
- **Show hints** and **Replay tutorial**.

## Accessibility

- **Reduced motion** gives a calmer camera, fewer effects and no decorative animation, also inside the panels. It defaults to your operating system's setting.
- **Text size** scales the whole interface.
- **Colour palette**: *Default* or *Colour-blind safe*. The safe palette is based on the Okabe-Ito set and recolours piste difficulty (sky blue, blue, vermillion, near-black), the quality and slope overlays, the slope profile and the good / bad / info colours, so they stay apart for the common kinds of colour blindness (and in greyscale).
- **Touch controls**: *Auto*, *On* or *Off*, see [Touch & tablets](page:touch).
- The game can be played largely from the keyboard (see [Keyboard shortcuts](page:shortcuts)), and dialogs trap and restore focus.

## Game

- **Month-end summary**: the small card showing income and expenses for the real month that just ended (*January*, *February*…).
- **Year-end report**: the Resort of the Year dialog; if off, the report is still in Finance → Reports.
- **Fast nights**: nights pass about three times faster.
- **Ride-along slow motion**.
- **Walk: mouse look**: *Drag* (default) or *Pointer lock*.

Equip unlocked cosmetics here. **Reset profile** clears achievements, cosmetics and scenario medals (never your saved games), after a confirmation.

## Privacy

{{fig:ui-settings-desktop}}

**Anonymous statistics** is an opt-in switch (off until you allow it): it lets the game send anonymous usage counts such as "a game was started" to Google Analytics. No names, saves or text are ever sent. The same choice is used on the website. Read what is and is not collected on the [privacy & analytics page](../../privacy/).
