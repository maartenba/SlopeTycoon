The mountain has weather you can see. None of it changes the game: guests, money and the rating behave exactly the same on a foggy morning and on a clear one. It is only about how the valley looks. The same day always looks the same, so reloading a save gives you the same morning.

## Valley fog

On some mornings a bank of fog fills the valley. It is most likely in the cold months, after rain and on cold clear mornings, and about one morning in six has one. Some of those are **inversion mornings**: the fog sits low in the valley while the high ground stays clear. The fog builds before sunrise, holds until about half past eight and is gone by about eleven. While it is there, the clock chip shows **fog** after the time.

{{fig:atmos-valley-fog}}

Buildings, guests and animals stay sharp inside the fog bank so the game remains easy to read. In the usual top-down view the fog looks like a thin haze over the land; it is much easier to see from **ride-along**, **walk** and **photo** views (see [Ride, Walk & Photo](page:modes)).

## Special sunrises and sunsets

About one sunrise or sunset in four is a pretty one. The sky warms up, the light turns golden, and the snow on the high ground catches a pink **alpenglow** for about forty minutes around the sun. It is a little more likely after a wet day and with broken cloud, and never in rain or a storm. Plain days stay plain on purpose; if every evening were orange, none of them would be special.

## Stars and the moon

After dark a clear sky has stars and a moon. The moon goes through its phases over about a month (a full cycle is 29.5 days of game time), so a full moon comes back every second game month. The sky is only visible at the screen edges in the top-down view, so stars and the moon are best in ride-along, walk and photo views.

## Cloud shadows, mist, frost and rainbows

- **Cloud shadows** drift slowly over the slopes on partly cloudy days.
- **Dawn mist** hangs low over lakes, ponds and rivers in the early morning (High only).
- **Frost** sparkles on the snow on cold clear mornings (High only).
- A **rainbow** can appear in the first clear spell after rain. It is rare, around one or two days in a hundred (High only).

## Photo mode suggestions

In **Photo mode** the Scene section offers **Golden hour**, **Foggy valley**, **Frosty morning** and **Starry night** buttons, but only when the current day really has that look. Each one just sets the photo's time of day; the game clock does not move. The saved PNG includes the stars, the moon, the fog and a rainbow if one is up.

## The Atmosphere setting

**Settings → Look and feel → Atmosphere** has three levels:

| Level | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Off** | Exactly the look of version 0.3.1: no fog banks, no special skies, no stars or moon. |
| **Low** | Valley fog (a little lighter), special skies with alpenglow, stars and moon, cloud shadows. |
| **High** | Everything above, plus dawn mist, frost sparkle and rainbows. |

The default is **High** on a desktop and **Low** on touch screens or when [Quality](page:settings) is set to Low. With **Reduced motion** on the picture is the same but everything holds still: no drifting fog, no twinkling stars.

> [!TIP] If the game runs slowly on an old laptop, turn Atmosphere to Low or Off. The fog adds a few calculations to every pixel of the land.
