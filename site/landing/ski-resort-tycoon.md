## What kind of game this is

If you are looking for a ski resort tycoon, this is the short version. You run a mountain resort from above, in 3D. You place lifts, paint pistes, lay out the village and watch small skiers use what you built. Guests pay for passes, food and rooms, and they leave a rating. The money you make pays for the next lift.

It plays in the browser, so it works on a laptop at work, on a school Chromebook or on a Mac, as long as WebGL is on. After your first visit it also works without a connection, and you can install it like an app.

## What you do

You start with a small valley. Every new game gives you a different valley. You raise and flatten terrain, lay a road, put a lift at the bottom of a slope and paint pistes down the hill, green, blue, red or black. Guests then arrive by car (and by train once you have built a station), buy a day pass, ride your lifts, ski the runs you painted, eat, rest and write a rating.

At first you only need that loop to work: a lift that people can reach, a piste that starts at the top station and ends near a path, somewhere to eat and somewhere to park. After that you widen it. More lifts, more runs, a ski school, a ranger station, snowmaking for the thin months, a hotel on a hill. If you want a plan to follow, [how to build a ski resort](lp:build-a-ski-resort) goes through it in order.

## If you remember Ski Resort Tycoon

I started playing [Ski Resort Tycoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ski_Resort_Tycoon) around the year 2000, after family ski holidays, to keep the week in the mountains going a bit longer. Slope Tycoon is inspired by that classic. It is not an official sequel, not a remake, and it does not copy it. It keeps the part I liked most, watching tiny skiers use the lift you placed, and adds 3D and seasons.

People also search for Ski Resort Tycoon 2 or a ski tycoon game for a modern machine. This is one answer to that, and I wrote an honest page about the others: [games like Ski Resort Tycoon](lp:games-like-ski-resort-tycoon).

## Winter and summer

The snow line follows the real calendar, so by April the high slopes still ski while the valley turns green. In summer your lifts carry hikers and mountain bikers instead, and you can build a campground, a petting zoo, a climbing crag, rafting on a river you dug yourself and a fishing pier. If you only build for winter, the money runs out in May. There is a longer look in [a mountain resort game for summer too](lp:summer-mountain-resort-game).

## What is in it

- **Twelve lift types**, from a T-bar to a ten-person gondola, a cable car and a funicular. [How ski lifts work](lp:how-ski-lifts-work) explains them.
- **Pistes are runs.** Every stretch between two junctions has a name, a difficulty and a traffic count, and guests choose between them.
- **Money that matters.** Day and season passes, marketing campaigns, loans and an energy bill. See [how money works](page:money).
- **Goals, scenarios and a yearly report.** Career mode levels your resort up and six scenarios give you a specific problem to solve.
- **Ride along, walk or photograph.** Follow a guest down a run, walk through your village in first person, or take a picture and save it as a PNG.
- **Almost no asset files.** The models and textures are made by code in your browser; sound effects and ambience mix recorded CC0 samples (mostly from Freesound, plus one clip of unknown origin) with sounds made in code; the music is a set of tracks generated with Suno, and there are two fonts.

## Free, offline, private

The game is free and has no ads. There are no accounts. If you click Allow on the consent bar, anonymous usage statistics are sent so I can see what is being played; if you do not, nothing is sent. The details are on the [privacy page](lp:privacy).

It is a pre-release, so expect rough edges and balance changes between versions. The [devlog](lp:devlog) lists what changed in each release.
