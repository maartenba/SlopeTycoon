## What you do

You start with a small valley. The mountain is generated from a seed, so every new game is a different shape. You raise and flatten terrain, lay a road, put a lift at the bottom of a slope and paint pistes down the hill, green, blue, red or black. Guests then arrive by car (and by train once you have built a station), buy a day pass, ride your lifts, ski the runs you painted, eat, rest and write a rating.

The first hour is about getting that loop to work: a lift that people can reach, a piste that starts at the top station and ends near a path, somewhere to eat and somewhere to park. After that you widen it. More lifts, more runs, a ski school, a ranger station, snowmaking for the thin months, a hotel on a hill.

## Winter and summer

Most resort games stop when the snow melts. In Slope Tycoon the snow line moves with the real calendar, so by April the high slopes still ski while the valley turns green. In summer your lifts carry hikers and mountain bikers instead. You can build a campground, a petting zoo, a climbing crag, rafting on a river you dug yourself, and a fishing pier. If you only build for winter you will watch the money run out in May. There is a longer look in [a mountain resort game for summer too](lp:summer-mountain-resort-game).

## If you remember Ski Resort Tycoon

I started playing [Ski Resort Tycoon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ski_Resort_Tycoon) around the year 2000, after family ski holidays, to keep the week in the mountains going a bit longer. This game is not a remake and does not copy it. It is what I would have wanted next: the same pleasure of watching tiny skiers use the lift you placed, in 3D, with seasons.

If you are looking for other options in this genre, I wrote an honest page: [games like Ski Resort Tycoon](lp:games-like-ski-resort-tycoon).

## What is in it

- **Twelve lift types**, from a T-bar to a ten-person gondola, a cable car and a funicular. [How ski lifts work](lp:how-ski-lifts-work) explains them.
- **Pistes are runs.** Every stretch between two junctions has a name, a difficulty and a traffic count, and guests choose between them.
- **Money that matters.** Day and season passes, marketing campaigns, loans and an energy bill. See [how money works](page:money).
- **Goals, scenarios and a yearly report.** Career mode levels your resort up and six scenarios give you a specific problem to solve.
- **Ride along, walk or photograph.** Follow a guest down a run, walk through your village in first person, or take a picture and save it as a PNG.
- **Everything is generated by code**, even the music and the sound effects. The only files are two fonts.

## Free, offline, private

The game is free and has no ads. After the first visit it works without a connection and you can install it like an app. There are no accounts. If you click Allow on the consent bar, anonymous usage statistics are sent so I can see what is being played; if you do not, nothing is sent. The details are on the [privacy page](lp:privacy).

It is a pre-release, so expect rough edges and balance changes between versions. The [devlog](lp:devlog) lists what changed in each release.
