## Three kinds of ski game

**Downhill and racing games.** You steer one skier or boarder down a mountain, usually against a clock. They reward quick reflexes, and the good ones teach you something about lines and speed. They are short, so they suit a break.

**Arcade and endless games.** You run down a slope and dodge trees, rocks and the odd monster. The old Windows ski game that many people remember belongs here. They are easy to pick up and have no end, and they are mostly played on a phone or a quick browser tab.

**Management games.** Here you do not ski at all, you run the resort. You decide where the lifts go, which slopes are green or black, what a ticket costs and whether the car park is big enough. Ski Resort Tycoon from around 2000 is the classic one. It is slower and it gives you a long game rather than a quick round.

## What "online" usually means

A browser ski game runs inside a web page: you open it, play and close the tab. Nothing is installed. The catch is that a browser game is often smaller or simpler than a store game, because the page has to load fast. A few are different. Slope Tycoon is a full 3D management game that loads in a tab, and once you have opened it, it works offline too.

If you want a quick round now, an arcade or racing game from a portal is the quickest. If you want something to play over an evening or several, a management game will last longer.

## Where Slope Tycoon fits

Slope Tycoon is the management kind. You raise and flatten terrain, place a lift, paint a piste down the hill and watch guests ride it. It uses the real calendar, so the snow line moves through the year and summer matters too. There are twelve lift types, from a T-bar to a gondola, and [how ski lifts work](lp:how-ski-lifts-work) explains the real machines behind them.

It is free, has no accounts or ads, and keeps saves on your device. It is a pre-release made by one person, so there are rough edges. You can [read more about it as a ski resort tycoon](lp:ski-resort-tycoon).
