## What counts here

I only include games you can open in a browser tab, play without paying and start without an account. "Free" in this page means free to start. Some browser games add paid extras later, so look at each one.

## Game portals

Sites such as CrazyGames, Poki and Coolmath Games have tycoon or management categories where you can browse hundreds of small games. Quality varies a lot, from polished to a weekend jam, so sort by popularity and play a few minutes before you commit. These are the easiest place to start if you just want something to open and try.

## itch.io

itch.io lets independent developers publish games that run in the page, and it has tags for tycoon, management and city builders. This is where I would look for odd, small and personal games that a portal does not feature. Many are free or pay-what-you-want. Read the comments before you start.

## Economy games that run online

Some tycoons are built to be played in a browser for a long time, with timers and a persistent world where you check in each day. They suit people who like slow progress, and they are a different thing from a game you play in one sitting. If you want that, search for "online economy game" and check how the game handles your account and your data first.

## Slope Tycoon

This is my own. It is a ski resort tycoon: you shape a mountain, build lifts and pistes, and look after guests for a whole year, including summer. It runs in a tab, works offline after the first visit, saves on your device and has no accounts or ads. It is a pre-release made by one person, so it is smaller than the big portals' showpieces, but there is a lot in it: twelve lift types, rivers, hotels, rafting, concerts and avalanche control.

## Picking a good one

- **Look at how it starts.** A good tycoon teaches you its loop quickly: build, earn, grow.
- **Check whether saves stay.** A browser game that loses your save when you close the tab is not much fun for a long session.
- **Watch for pressure to pay.** Timers and premium currency are a sign the game is built around spending.
- **Read what it says about data.** Fewer permissions is better.
