## Start small, on purpose

New resorts start with $150,000 and one winter ahead. That sounds like a lot until a gondola costs half of it. Choose Career, a small map, Normal difficulty and a winter start for the first game. The tutorial hints can be switched off later.

Before you build anything, look at the valley. A lift wants a steady slope that is not too steep, close to the road, and a run that can come down beside it. Flat ground near the lodge is for parking and the village. You can always reshape the terrain, but it costs money, so look first.

## 1. The lift

Open the Lifts drawer and pick a T-bar or a 2-seat chairlift. They are cheap, they carry enough people for a start, and you can replace them later. Click the bottom station, then the top. If the game says the line is too steep, or the cable would hit the ground, move one end a little. [How ski lifts work](lp:how-ski-lifts-work) explains what separates a T-bar from a gondola, and the guide has a page on [clearance and towers](page:lifts-pistes).

## 2. The piste

Paint a green piste from the top station down to a path near the bottom. A piste has to start at a lift station to count, and a long, gentle one is better at first than a short steep one. Guests choose by difficulty, so your first runs should be easy. Add a blue run once the green one is busy. The Runs tab shows traffic for each stretch, which tells you where to build next.

## 3. The village

Guests walk from the lodge to the bottom station, so draw a footpath between them. Then add the basics: parking, a ski patrol hub, a first-aid hut and somewhere to eat. Buildings without a connection are marked as not connected, and they earn nothing. Toilets matter more than you would think. Guests who cannot find one say so in their rating.

## 4. Grow one thing at a time

Press play and watch for a while. Money comes from passes, food and rooms. When cash allows, add a second lift that serves new ground, or a longer run that spreads the crowd. Ranger Rudi, the advice badge in the corner, reads your actual resort and tells you what looks short. He never spends for you.

A hotel on a hill is a late step. So is a train station. Do not build them before the lifts earn money.

## 5. Snow, and then summer

When natural snow gets thin, build a reservoir and a few snowmaking cannons and use the overlay to place them where the runs are busiest. After the first winter, look at the summer. Your lifts carry hikers and mountain bikers, and a campground or a rafting base gives them something to do. A resort that sleeps from April to November runs low on cash. There is [more on the summer half](lp:summer-mountain-resort-game) if you want it.
